# Rule digest

1. Plunder fights another player's truck: PvP, a fixed red line; no seat plunders. OD-20261123-03 (decided, relayed by the Steward) bars PvP and attack, overriding the articles' plunder target rule and quota (how-it-p1-2, limits-section, day-20261030-p1).
2. Non-UR days dispatch SR or above: as rolled; below SR, free refresh, then Trade Contract refreshes (owner-authorized 2026-10-26); never diamonds on refreshes (how-it-is-p1, limits-section).
3. UR duel days: Mega Mode batch-dispatches all four slots, UR only; a UR dispatch reads +300,000 (how-it-is-p1, how-it-is-p2, open-questions-p1).
4. Article order: settle returned trucks first, then dispatch to 5 of 5; trust on-screen counters; the day resets 02:00 UTC (day-20261030-p1).
5. A returned truck settles when its Truck Details open; verify paging on the screenshot; claim Reward Recovery at the red dot (how-rewards-are-p1, how-rewards-are-p2).
6. Find the trade icon by glyph and badge each unit; verify the screen that opened (how-to-enter-p1, how-to-enter-p4).
7. 2026-11-22: trade screen never opened; last live counters 2026-11-11, stale; change method (run-20261122-0252-p1).
8. Armed Truck loot belongs to resource-collection (what-this-activity).

Unverified: OD-20261123-03 (entry in _records_/retro/owner-decisions-log/od-20261123-01.md, no own file) does not name trucks; reading plunder as a fight is the articles' wording; OD-20261123-05 (relayed by the Steward) says it stands. Tap points are old ticker measures (how-to-enter-p1).

# Rule digest

1. Claiming reward attachments is in scope; Delete All is never pressed (cadence-section, _records_/2026-11-22/mail-rewards/0619-queue-run.md).
2. Purchases and diamond spend stay red lines; In-app Purchases holds receipts only, nothing to claim (how-to-enter-p1, _records_/2026-11-11/mail-rewards/1029-queue-run.md).
3. A category red dot means unread, not claimable; only a gift icon evidences a claim (run-20261103-1740-p1, _records_/2026-11-09/mail-rewards/0138-queue-run.md).
4. Claim All answers "No rewards available." when nothing is claimable; verify a claim by gift icons gone, green checkmarks or HUD deltas, not a missing toast (_records_/2026-11-04/mail-rewards/0212-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-08/mail-rewards/2132-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-11/mail-rewards/1029-queue-run.md).
5. A second Claim All after a list is claimed opens the next unread mail; check what opened (how-to-enter-p2, _records_/2026-11-11/zombie-invasion/1456-queue-run.md).
6. A non-mail unit opens no mail detail in background control unless a relaunch is budgeted (_records_/2026-11-11/zombie-invasion/1456-queue-run.md, run-20261111-2136).
7. TIPS notifications popup at Mail close: tap Cancel, never Enable Now (_records_/2026-11-22/mail-rewards/0619-queue-run.md).
8. Category rail read live 2026-11-11: Battle Report, Alliance, Event, System, Regular, In-app Purchases, Favorite (_records_/2026-11-11/mail-rewards/1422-queue-run.md).
9. Latest read 2026-11-22: full sweep, every Claim All answered "No rewards available.", nothing claimed (_records_/2026-11-22/mail-rewards/0619-queue-run.md).
10. Entry, unverified (old ticker units): ground the Mail envelope fresh each unit; confirm Mail, not Alliance, opened (how-to-enter-p1, run-20261102-0840, run-20261103-1740-p1).

Unverified: tap offsets, normalized coordinates, window or fullscreen framing and the mail-detail back-arrow re-test (old ticker units, not the computer-use bridge); which reward mails expire (open-questions).

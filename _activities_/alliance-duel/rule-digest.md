# Rule digest

1. Never close a duel check without looking for the ceremony trophy icon on the base screen; 2026-11-22 misread it as absent (what-this-activity, run-20261122-1400-p2).
2. The weekly claim is the Star Ceremony, not the VS duel screens; it costs nothing to play (what-this-activity, scope-and-availability).
3. Ceremony: heart first, then forward; owner procedure: hearts for at least 3 winners (the-star-ceremony, run-20261122-1400-p2).
4. Forwarding too fast can skip award cards; confirm position by screenshot, never by the receipt alone (the-star-ceremony).
5. Owner target: 3,600,000 personal points per duel day, read live on the VS panel; fragments never substitute (day-20261031, day-20261101-duel-p1).
6. The duel day rolls about 18:00 local, not 19:00; the closing read is the last before then (cadence-section, day-20261031, day-20261101-duel-p2).
7. A duel-score unit is read-only: record target, live total, gap; points 0, never a running total (day-20261101-duel-p1, day-20261111).
8. An unreadable total is "total unread", never estimated; settlement day shows none (day-20261101-duel-p2, run-20261122-1400-p1).
9. A tracking unit never buys to chase the target (day-20261101-duel-p2, scope-and-availability).

Unverified: what the chest counter and Go button do (the-star-ceremony, run-20261108-0150-p1); why the icon vanished unfinished (run-20261108-2218-p1); Event 42 finale claim (run-20261122-1400-p1); normalized-coordinate taps, old ticker units, not the current bridge (run-20261108-0150-p1).

# Alliance duel

## What this activity is

The weekly Alliance Duel event, where this alliance faces one rival
alliance across themed days and members earn points from matching
activities. This folder accumulates duel knowledge - the day themes,
how points are earned, and which chests can be claimed passively.
Grinding points by spending resources or speedups is a decision for
the owner, not part of a claim check.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/<date>/alliance-duel/`, one file per execution.

## How to enter

To be confirmed in game on 2026-10-25. Expected route - Events from
the base screen, then the Alliance Duel banner.

## Cadence

- Weekly cycle, expected to run Monday to Saturday with a results
  and reward day, exact reset times to be confirmed in game.
- Each day has a theme; points earned that day feed daily chests and
  the weekly alliance total.

## Scope and availability

- Alliance versus alliance, matchmaking by alliance power is expected.
- Personal chests open at point thresholds; alliance rewards depend
  on the weekly result.
- Not season limited as far as is known.

## Open questions

- The exact weekly schedule, day themes and reset hour.
- This week opponent, current score and chest state.

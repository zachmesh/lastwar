- RELEASE posture - every pin is worked to a closing read
  in the unit: tapped and completed, claimed, or identified
  with the reason it is left. "Pins left unattempted" is a
  partial, never a done - the 2026-10-29 18:47 and 22:21
  units both closed partial on exactly this, and the pins
  were still standing the next day. (2026-11-11 0922 update: 9 pins inventoried at entry - 3 grey skulls with red dots, 1 blue person with a red dot, 1 centre purple pin ([tag]Starfall), 4 green/ally portrait pins at the top (the 3 top-centre portraits may be alliance-member markers rather than task pins) - and 0 standing at close, with NO pin tapped in the unit. The pins vanished between the first CONGRATULATIONS screenshot (pins visible behind the popup) and the post-restart screenshot. Candidates, none verified: the Claim All pool included pin rewards; the pin tasks expired; the app restart re-rendered without them. This breaks the Issue #632 pattern (release units closing partial on unworked pins) in the sense that no pin remains standing, but the clearing mechanism is UNVERIFIED - a release unit closing done on 0 pins standing without working any pin is NOT proof the pins were claimed. The next release unit watches whether pins reappear with the 02:21:35 restore. The 0504 unit this morning also found pins that opened no card (1 pin, 3 tap variants, no card), so at least some pins in this cycle were non-interactive.)
- STACK posture - do NOT tap pins. A finished-task pin pays
  instantly on tap and leaks the claim onto a non-scoring
  day. Instead the unit records a pin inventory (class if
  known, position, red dot or not) as part of its closing
  read, so the release-day unit inherits a checklist rather
  than a surprise.
- Either posture - the pin scan is part of the closing read
  of every radar unit. No scan, no done.

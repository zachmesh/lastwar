# Radar tasks

## What this activity is

Clearing radar tasks - claiming stored rewards first, then spending
stamina on Quick Execute batches. This folder accumulates radar
knowledge - storage cap, stamina costs and reward patterns.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/activity-executions/radar-tasks.md`.

## How it is done

- Open the radar from the satellite icon on the base screen.
- Claim All for completed stored tasks first.
- Run Quick Execute batches, then Claim All after each batch.
- A TIPS confirmation appears on Claim All - Confirm it. The do not show
  me today box was ticked on 2026-10-25.
- Map pins that need a march or a fight are a separate decision, not part
  of Quick Execute.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- Radar level 12, storage cap 40. Stored tasks fell from 34 of 40 to
  6 of 40 in one round.
- Batches run were 40, 30 and 20 stamina. Stamina ended at 12, with full
  restore shown as 2026-10-26 04:51:41 local.
- First Claim All paid out chests, radar tokens, Upgrade Ore, speedups,
  Hero EXP, coin, iron and food as itemised in the day play record.
- Twelve tasks were shown as restoring in about 5 hours 22 minutes.
- Remaining map pins were mostly ally assist, digging and combat tasks.

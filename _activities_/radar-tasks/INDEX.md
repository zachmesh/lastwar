# Radar tasks

## What this activity is

Clearing radar tasks - claiming stored rewards first, then spending
stamina on Quick Execute batches, and finally handling the special
map pin tasks that Quick Execute does not cover. This folder
accumulates radar knowledge - storage cap, stamina costs, the
stamina regain panel and the special task flow.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/<date>/radar-tasks/`, one file per execution.

## How to enter

- From the base or the world map, tap the satellite dish icon in the
  left icon column (about norm 50,695). It may carry a red badge with
  the number of tasks ready.
- The radar screen shows radar level and points top left, stored
  tasks out of 40, a stamina lightning figure top right, the restore
  countdown, and map pins for the tasks themselves.

## Cadence

- Stored tasks restore over time - 12 tasks were shown restoring in
  about 2 hours 37 minutes on the evening of 2026-10-25, with full
  restore at 2026-10-26 22:51:41 local time.
- Each ordinary task costs 10 stamina through Quick Execute.
- The free stamina claim in the Regain Stamina panel has a 4 hour
  cooldown and can be used twice a day.

## Scope and availability

- Radar level 12 and storage cap 40 on this account.
- Stamina cap 120, regenerating 1 point every 300 seconds.
- Special pin tasks are season themed - the one completed so far is
  a Season II winter task. How often special pins appear is not yet
  known.

## Red dots and red badges - the owner rule

- A red badge number on a tab or icon means that many tasks or
  claims are ready to do there (owner, 2026-10-25).
- A red dot on a radar map pin means that pin is clickable and its
  task can be done now (owner, 2026-10-25). Do not treat a pin with
  a red dot as decoration - open it.

## Pin tap caution - finished tasks pay on tap, 2026-10-29

- A red-dotted map pin is not always a special task. Twice
  on 2026-10-29 (a blue person pin at 02:24, a purple skull
  pin at 14:52) tapping the pin opened a CONGRATULATIONS
  popup and claimed a finished task at once, with no card
  and no chance to back out. On a stack-posture day that
  claim runs against the posture, so do not tap map pins
  casually while stacking - inspect the pin type first
  from the record history, and expect an instant claim.
- Entry grounding, 2026-10-29 - the left-column dish icon
  with its red badge sits at norm 62,727 on a fresh
  screenshot. The earlier 54,667 estimate opened Secret
  Command Post in the 06:44 and 11:19 units; use 62,727
  first.

## How ordinary tasks are done

- Claim All for completed stored tasks first when the bottom bar
  offers it.
- Run Quick Execute - the bottom bar states how many tasks can be
  completed and the stamina required. After the marches the bar
  changes to Claim All with the number of rewards waiting.
- Claim All after each batch. A TIPS confirmation appears on Claim
  All - Confirm it. The do not show me today box was ticked on
  2026-10-25.
- When the bottom bar disappears, ordinary tasks are done - but a
  special pin may still be standing. Always scan the map for pins
  before calling the radar clear.

## Regain Stamina panel

- Tapping Quick Execute with too little stamina opens the REGAIN
  STAMINA panel instead of failing.
- Free option - Claim 75 stamina, cooldown 4 hours, twice a day.
  Always take the free claim first.
- Paid option - Buy 100 stamina for 300 rubies. Never use it.
- Item options - 10 stamina items (600 owned) and 50 stamina items
  (589 owned) with quantity selectors. Not used so far.

## Special pin tasks - how to complete them

Special tasks sit on the radar map as pins (a purple question mark
pin so far) and are NOT covered by Quick Execute. Verified flow,
from A Distressed Survivor on 2026-10-25 evening -

1. Tap the pin on the radar map. A bottom card opens with the task
   title, a countdown (about 7 hours 56 minutes remaining), the
   reward area and a Go button. Rewards may be hidden behind a
   state line - here "Break the ice to view rewards".
2. Tap Go. The view jumps to the task location on the world map
   and the card shows the situation state - here Thawing Soon with
   a timer, a snowflake bar at 1,000 of 1,000, and one large round
   contextual action button (a hammer here).
3. Tap the contextual button. A march panel opens - squad, power
   versus Unknown, a match rating, march time and a stamina cost
   (5 here). Tap March. The squad marches (March Queues 1 of 4,
   back to 0 of 4 on arrival) and the situation resolves - the ice
   broke and the survivor stood free on the map.
4. Go back to the radar screen (satellite icon) and tap the same
   pin again. The card has changed - the reward list is now
   revealed (here a survivor times 1, a paw print item times 200
   and black rock times 207.3K).
5. Tap Go again. On the world map the card is simplified and the
   round button is now a green magnifier.
6. Tap the magnifier. A story conversation opens with a large
   character portrait. Just keep tapping the bottom of the dialog
   to advance it to the end - the owner instruction is to complete
   the conversation with clicks only. If a chat screen opens by an
   extra tap, the Back Arrow at the bottom left exits it.
7. Return to the radar screen. The bottom bar now reads "1 task
   rewards can be claimed" with a Claim All button. Claim All pays
   the revealed rewards (here paw print 200, black rock 207.3K,
   survivor 1) and adds 1 radar point. The pin and its red dot
   disappear - the radar is fully clear.

## Doom pins - Legion base and Walker rally, 2026-10-25 late

Two further pin types completed in the late round -

- Doom Legion base pin - tapping it jumps to a legion base on the
  world map (level 120, suggested power about 20.1M). Attacking it
  destroyed it outright with no return march to wait for. Loot -
  food 1.4M, iron 1.4M and a coin amount read between 0.9M and 1.7M.
- Doom Walker pin - a level 160 world boss, suggested power
  21,454,000, with a daily first kill reward. Flow - pin card, Go,
  then Initiate Rally on the boss panel and again on the rally
  panel (rally time 1 minute default, the fight resolves after the
  squad marches, about 7 minutes here). Rally stamina cost 20.
- Rally launch warning - the first launch looked dispatched but
  cost no stamina and produced no march, a silent no-op. A launch
  is only real when the stamina drops by 20 and the march timer
  appears; verify both before waiting. The kill credited back at
  the radar as claimable task rewards.

## Gathering pins - verified 2026-10-26

- Some purple pins are gathering spots, not story tasks - a
  Diamond Mine pin and a Rare Gold Mine pin stood together on
  the 10:03 run map. The pin card names the spot, its rewards
  and a despawn timer of about 3 hours, with a Go button.
- Flow - Go jumps to the vein on the world map (a story dialog
  may play first; advance it). Tap the big round pickaxe
  button to open the march panel - a full squad is
  preselected, the gathering amount equals the vein size, and
  no stamina cost is shown (none was deducted either run).
  Gathering speed bonuses on these veins are extreme (plus
  100 percent on the Lv.24 Diamond Vein, plus 20000 percent
  on the Lv.28 Gold Mine), so gathering took 00:02:11 and
  00:09:20 for 200 and 1,080,000 units.
- Claiming - when the diamond squad returned, the radar page
  showed the task as claimable and Claim All paid the pin
  rewards (red gem 20, blue swirl item 10). The gold pin was
  claimed by tapping the finished pin, which opened the
  rewards popup directly (coin 73.6K, blue swirl item 10).
  Each claimed pin paid plus 1 radar point (2,832 to 2,834
  across both). The gathered yield itself (1.0M coin) lands
  in the HUD when the squad returns, separate from the pin
  reward.

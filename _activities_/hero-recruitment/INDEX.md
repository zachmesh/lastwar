# Hero recruitment

## What this activity is

Taking the free recruitment pull on each banner when its timer is ready.
This folder accumulates recruitment knowledge - banners, free pull
timers, pity and wish counters. Spending tickets beyond the free pulls
needs the owner approval. Executions are recorded in
`_records_/<date>/hero-recruitment/`, one file per execution.

## How it is done

- Open recruitment and check each banner for a ready free pull.
- Take one free pull per ready banner and record the result and counters.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- S2 Legend Returns banner - free pull gave a McGregor Shard times 1.
  Its pity counter showed 3 of 50, tickets 46, next free pull in about
  7 hours.
- Recruit Heroes banner - free pull gave a Swift Shard times 1. Wish
  progress showed 11 of 200, pity 49 of 50, tickets 105.
- No tickets were spent beyond the two free pulls.

# Rule digest

1. Enter from the Events hub, Survey Center tab (red dot badge 1 while a survey is open); the tab also shows in Special Ops when surveys exist (how-it-is, what-this-activity).
2. Read the card (title, Proceed, countdown if timed) and the Instructions (i button) for the survey's rules (how-it-is, run-20261109-1117-known).
3. Proceed opens an external survey link in the owner's Chrome; no agent answers or submits anything, since answering is the owner's own opinion (how-it-is, run-20261109-1117-known).
4. The path is free: no diamonds, no money, no priced button. It pays a completion reward, not points. Purchases and diamond spend stay red lines (run-20261109-1117-known, what-this-activity, OD-20261123-05).
5. Each survey submits once; exiting midway is fine and Continue resumes (how-it-is).
6. Timed surveys vanish at countdown end, untimed ones 3 hours after completion; a completed survey's unclaimed reward is mailed (how-it-is, run-20261109-1117-known).
7. Old route, unverified: a Proceed tap can open Chrome while the game window shows no change; check the window list before re-tapping (run-20261109-1117-known).

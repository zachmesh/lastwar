# Rule digest

1. Take every survivor in; recruiting is free (scope-and-availability).
2. Tap the figure, not the bubble; crop the gate and confirm "?" and figure first (how-to-enter-p2, how-to-enter-p5).
3. Not survivors: pet markers, chat-bar bubbles, tent face bubbles, "!" visitors (how-to-enter-p3, how-to-enter-p4, how-to-enter-p5).
4. A smiley or a cleared "?" is processed state, not a verified recruit; with no card read, record it unverified (how-a-recruit-p1).
5. The line is clear only when no "?" bubbles remain; never presumed from an empty gate; if unverifiable, close unverified and name a re-read unit (how-a-recruit-p1, day-20261031-p1).
6. Re-verify the line before tapping a second figure (how-a-recruit-p2).
7. The gate sits at the bottom edge behind the chat bar; pan down; the banner can cover figures (how-to-enter-p2).
8. After an intake, Quick Dispatch is the next unit's first action (R7): tap the building body, then the paw; the centre tap is retired (scope-and-availability, day-20261031-p1, day-20261031-p5).
9. Never press Escape; it quit the game (day-20261101).
10. The fourth identical partial is a process failure reported to the owner (R2) (day-20261031-p1).

Unverified: Quick Dispatch effect, 24 consecutive units unverified (day-20261031-p5, changelog-section-p1); window-state and move-wait-click methods, from ticker units, not the current bridge (how-to-enter-p1, how-a-recruit-p2); line refill rate (open-questions-p1).

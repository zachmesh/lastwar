# Rule digest

1. Open the queue from the flask icon and read whichever screen opens, list panel or research tree; the bottom bar shows both slots (how-it-is-p1).
2. Ground the flask centre from a fresh crop; never tap its left edge (how-it-is-p1).
3. Read both slots live; carry no research name from an older read (day-20261025-p2, run-20261122-1010-p1).
4. Press Complete on any finished item and record the power change (how-it-is-p2, what-this-activity).
5. A scout unit only observes; the completion belongs to the research unit (day-20261025-p1).
6. Starting new research is the owner's direction; report idle slots (what-this-activity, run-20261122-1010-p1).
7. Speed Up is read, never tapped (run-20261122-1010-p1).
8. Bottom-bar taps are passive readouts (how-it-is-p1).

Unverified: whether the flask tap lands on the list panel or the tree, which varies by session; tap points measured by ticker units, not the current bridge (how-it-is-p1).

# Rule digest

1. Open the queue from the flask icon and read whichever screen opens, list panel or research tree; the bottom bar shows both slots (how-it-is-p1).
2. Ground the flask centre from a fresh crop; never tap its left edge (how-it-is-p1).
3. Read both slots live; carry no research name from an older read (day-20261025-p2, _records_/2026-11-22/research-queue/1010-queue-run.md).
4. Press Complete on any finished item and record the power change (how-it-is-p2, what-this-activity).
5. A scout unit only observes; the completion belongs to the research unit (day-20261025-p1).
6. Idle labs are filled under the growth start rule (see Errata); an idle slot at a read is recorded (what-this-activity, _records_/2026-11-22/research-queue/1010-queue-run.md).
7. Speed Up is read, never tapped (_records_/2026-11-22/research-queue/1010-queue-run.md).
8. Bottom-bar taps are passive readouts (how-it-is-p1).

9. A blocked slot closes with slots_blocked_by_capacity=<N> and capacity_blocker="<text>" (for example "Drill Ground Full"); a count without a blocker exempts nothing (`_tools_/strategy-conformance/INDEX.md`).
Unverified: whether the flask tap lands on the list panel or the tree, which varies by session; tap points measured by ticker units, not the current bridge (how-it-is-p1). Errata: [errata](errata.md).

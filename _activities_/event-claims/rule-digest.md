# Rule digest

1. Recenter the base view first; the green up-arrow is a retract control (how-it-is, run-20261122-0253).
2. Sweep every hub tab: wait after each tap, drag the bar to its end (how-it-is, day-20261101).
3. Claim earned rewards; Doomsday first-kill tiers re-arm each round (how-it-is, run-20261108-0656-p1).
4. A bouncing chest or reward dot is unclaimed until opened; rescan after claiming; check each reward set (how-it-is, day-20261026).
5. A tab dot or badge is not claim proof (run-20261102-2341, run-20261108-1506-p1).
6. Priced buttons are never claims: Battle Pass unlock, Legendary Claim All, Claim More (standing-limits).
7. Level Swap: inspect only per the articles (see Errata); registration Sign Up and surveys untouched (standing-limits, run-20261101-2200-p1, run-20261110-0519-p1).
8. Go (attack) and Challenge, a mini game, are grinding, not claims: keep them out of claim rounds, never against a player (run-20261101-1055, run-20261108-1506-p1).
9. Age of Oil milestone chests are claimed (run-20261109-0450-p1, run-20261111-2147).
10. Never send Esc; it crashes the app (unverified, old channel) (run-20261102-0359, run-20261108-1935).

Unverified: red circular-arrow recenter control (how-it-is); Age of Oil stage-1 badge claim (run-20261111-0305-p1); hub state after 2026-11-11 (run-20261122-0253).

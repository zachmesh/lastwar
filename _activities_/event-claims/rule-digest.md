# Rule digest

1. Recenter the base view first; the green up-arrow is a retract control (how-it-is, run-20261122-0253).
2. Sweep every hub tab: wait after each tap, drag the bar to its end (how-it-is, day-20261101).
3. Claim earned rewards; Doomsday first-kill tiers re-arm each round (how-it-is, _records_/2026-11-08/event-claims/0656-queue-run.md).
4. A bouncing chest or reward dot is unclaimed until opened; rescan after claiming; check each reward set (how-it-is, day-20261026).
5. A tab dot or badge is not claim proof (_records_/2026-11-02/event-claims/2341-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-08/event-claims/1506-queue-run.md).
6. Priced buttons are never claims: Battle Pass unlock, Legendary Claim All, Claim More (standing-limits).
7. Level Swap: inspect only per the articles (see Errata); registration Sign Up and surveys untouched (standing-limits, _records_/2026-11-01/event-claims/2200-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-10/event-claims/0519-queue-run.md).
8. Go (attack) and Challenge, a mini game, are grinding, not claims: keep them out of claim rounds, never against a player (_records_/2026-11-01/event-claims/1055-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-08/event-claims/1506-queue-run.md).
9. Age of Oil milestone chests are claimed (_records_/2026-11-09/event-claims/0450-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-11/arms-race/2147-queue-run.md).
10. Never send Esc; it crashes the app (unverified, old channel) (_records_/2026-11-02/event-claims/0359-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-08/radar-tasks/2028-queue-run.md).

Unverified: red circular-arrow recenter control (how-it-is); Age of Oil stage-1 badge claim (_records_/2026-11-11/event-claims/0305-queue-run.md); hub state after 2026-11-11 (run-20261122-0253).

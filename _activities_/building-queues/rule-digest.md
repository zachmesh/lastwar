# Rule digest

1. Open the build queue list from the hammer icon; record each upgrade's level and time left (what-this-activity).
2. Start the Builders Hut Lv.25 to 26 upgrade: iron 170M, food 56.9M, no diamonds (OD-20261109-02, day-20261025-p2).
3. Standing: when stocks of food, iron and coin are at least 3x the cost, an idle builder starts the highest-power upgrade needing no diamonds and no speed-up; priority HQ, then Builders Hut, then military; re-read builders each play (OD-20261109-02).
4. Never tap Finish Now, spend diamonds or use a speed-up (day-20261025-p2, OD-20261109-02).
5. The green up arrow is an upgrade shortcut; never use it to close a panel (day-20261025-p2).
6. Close building panels from their base area with care (what-this-activity).
7. The live panel read stands over any older read (day-20261025-p1).

Unverified: rules 2 to 4 rest on the owner's answer as relayed by the coordinator, not yet logged; the hammer tap point, measured by ticker units, not the current bridge (what-this-activity); whether the Drill Ground upgrade raises troop capacity (open-questions); whether the Builders Hut quest was completed or rotated (day-20261025-p2).

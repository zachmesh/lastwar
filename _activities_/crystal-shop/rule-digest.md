# Rule digest

1. Until the tab is located: open the Store from the base screen, enter the Building section, not Hot Deals, open the Crystal Shop tab and read it live (the-job-until).
2. Claim everything claimable under the red lines: no spending of diamonds, rubies or money without owner approval (the-job-until).
3. Once located, record what the tab is, how crystal is collected and its cadence in the same pass (the-job-until).
4. Ruled out: the Hot Deals tab row, which has no Building or Crystal Shop tab; the Diamond tab, purchase packs only; the MALL header, a screen title (run-20261101-1241, _records_/2026-11-02/crystal-shop/0446-queue-run.md).
5. The untried route: check whether the Building section sits outside the cart shop, as a base-screen building panel, learn-only (run-20261101-1241, open-questions).
6. Crystal items in mail rewards are not Crystal Shop progress; never merge them in a report (what-is-known).
7. Until located, name the item in every round summary, age counted from 2026-10-25 (what-this-activity, the-job-until).
8. Back arrows differ per screen; re-ground each one (_records_/2026-11-02/crystal-shop/0446-queue-run.md).

Unverified: the tab itself, never opened (open-questions); the patch-note lead "Store, Building section" against the current UI (what-is-known); tap and drag points, from old ticker units, not the current bridge (_records_/2026-11-02/crystal-shop/0446-queue-run.md).

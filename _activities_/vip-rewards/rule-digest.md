# Rule digest

1. Claim the Daily Freebie and the login chest independently, each with a live points read before and after (how-to-enter, cadence-section).
2. The freebie unlocks at the 18:00 daily reset (02:00Z), one per reset day; read the button or countdown live (cadence-section, day-20261109, day-20261119).
3. Never close a round without opening the VIP screen (cadence-section).
4. No dot, no popup and no points change means claimed only if the VIP screen was read; a failed unit proves nothing (_records_/2026-11-02/vip-rewards/2138-queue-run.md, run-20261106-1634).
5. A popularity popup can cover the profile: dismiss it with the plain Awesome! button (how-to-enter, _records_/2026-11-07/vip-rewards/1830-queue-run.md).
6. Dismiss the chest popup by the dimmed area, not the X (_records_/2026-11-02/vip-rewards/0438-queue-run.md).
7. The freebie-button blocker is not a standing state; a new failure is a new observation (day-20261109, run-20261122-1522-p2).
8. Purchases are a fixed red line, never an operational choice: no VIP points or level bought with diamonds or money; leave the VIP Exclusive offer, points plus button, cart shortcut and VIP Store untouched (what-this-activity, scope-and-availability, _records_/2026-11-08/perception-scout/0542-queue-run.md).
9. The freebie pays items; the chest pays the day's VIP points (run-20261122-1522-p1).

Unverified: tap methods, measured by old ticker units, not the current computer-use bridge (how-to-enter, _records_/2026-11-02/vip-rewards/0438-queue-run.md); freebie rotation and VIP 14 rewards (open-questions).

# Season warming

## What this activity is

Assisting allies to warm their bases in Season 2 Polar Storm. This folder
accumulates warming knowledge - costs, targets and season task progress.
Background mechanics live in `_knowledge_/season-two-winter`.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/activity-executions/season-warming.md`.

## How it is done

- From the Season page or an ally base, choose a warming assist.
- Confirm each assist one at a time and count them to the target.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- Each assist cost Coal times 100 per Confirm.
- Assists on ally [AP3x]Amarky257 were completed 10 of 10.
- Season task progress afterwards read warming 10 of 15 as displayed.
- The own furnace Overdrive was not enabled to chase the count, because
  Overdrive burns coal at four times the normal rate per the season notes.

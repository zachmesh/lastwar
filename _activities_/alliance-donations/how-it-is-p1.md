## How it is done

- Donate to the current target technology with the coin button.
- The recommended marker is the small round red-panda badge on the node's tile (2026-11-08 2350: Rallied Attack the only tile carrying one in the full War-tab read). (2026-11-10 0747 update: the badge has MOVED - it now sits on the Troop Movement tile, not on Rallied Attack. The marker moves between units; read it fresh every unit and never assume Rallied Attack.) The red thumbs-up icon sits fixed above the Development tab and does not move with the tree scroll - it is NOT a node marker, so do not read it as one. Prior sections attributing the thumbs-up to a node record what was observed then; the Development-tab marker form is unverified and needs a fresh read. (2026-11-11 1004 update: the badge has MOVED again - it now sits on the Unit Load Capacity tile, read fresh this unit; it was on Rallied Attack at the 0733 unit this morning. The marker moves between units; read it fresh every unit and never assume Rallied Attack. Coin cost varies by node: 300 on Unit Load Capacity (this unit) against 800 on Rallied Attack and 1000 on Quick Construction - read the cost on the panel, never carry one.) (2026-11-22 update: CORRECTION - Quick Construction coin Donate is 1000, not 900: read live 1000 in the 0113, 0319 and 0441 units; Rallied Attack 800 confirmed in the 0554 and 0948 units, Quick Gathering 300 in the 1308 unit. The 900 figure carried since the 2026-11-11 1004 update is superseded. The 0359 unit section's result-stats line and the 1004 unit section's comparison still quote the carried 900 - both are stale carry, not live reads; see the 2026-11-22 fold section.)
- The diamond button is never used without the owner approval, under the
  recording rules on spending.
- Donate in every run, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - alliance tech
  donation is part of the regular routine, not a once a day check.
  Each run opens Alliance Techs and, whenever coin donations still
  remain today, donates them to the recommended technology until
  the daily coin count is used up. If the recommended technology
  completes mid way, follow the new recommended marker. If no coin
  donations remain, record the visit as checked only. Claim the
  daily donation reward whenever it shows as claimable.
- Verify the count after EVERY tap, not just every batch - measured 2026-11-07 (2018 unit): a single tap on the green coin button was eaten by the grey-out, proven live by waiting out its 00:02:23 timer and re-reading the count unchanged at 1/30.

# Alliance gifts

## What this activity is

Claiming the gifts the alliance generates for its members. This folder
accumulates gift knowledge - gift levels, tiers and claim flow.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/<date>/alliance-gifts/`, one file per execution.

## How it is done

- Open Alliance, then the gifts entry.
- Claim every available gift, noting the tier split shown on screen.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- The alliance gift level shown was 28.
- The claim that day read 844 plus 2 Regular and 72 Premium, as displayed.
  What the 844 figure counts is not yet confirmed.

# Rule digest

1. One tap on a completed row's Claim claims every completed row in view, and a chest tap every claimable chest; claimed rows sink with green checks, not removed (what-this-activity, _records_/2026-11-09/daily-missions/1519-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-09/daily-missions/1519-queue-run.md, _records_/2026-11-08/daily-missions/1753-queue-run.md).
2. Never keep tapping Claim on a reordered list without a fresh read; a stale tap can land on Go (what-this-activity, day-20261025).
3. An open chest shows contents only and spends nothing; 40-chest receipts vary across reset days (_records_/2026-11-09/daily-missions/1519-queue-run.md, run-20261122-1504-p2).
4. Pay nothing: all five chests are reachable without the paid-purchase row; it is observed only (_records_/2026-11-08/perception-scout/0229-queue-run.md, run-20261122-1504-p2).
5. Attack a Commander is PvP and stays undone; Plunder others' Secret Task reward is listed 0/1, never tapped. Both are held under the PvP red line (OD-20261123-03 in _records_/retro/owner-decisions-log/od-20261123-01.md; _records_/2026-11-08/perception-scout/0229-queue-run.md, run-20261122-1504-p2).
6. Report tasks needing combat, gathering marches or other regulars as not done (what-this-activity, _records_/2026-11-09/daily-missions/1519-queue-run.md).
7. Next Refresh points at the 18:00 local reset (_records_/2026-11-08/perception-scout/0229-queue-run.md).
8. Name no unseen rows; the activity star's composition is open (open-questions-p4).

6. Report tasks needing combat, gathering marches or other regulars as not done (what-this-activity, run-20261109-1519-p3).
7. Next Refresh points at the 18:00 local reset (run-20261108-0229-p2).
8. Name no unseen rows; the activity star's composition is open (open-questions-p4).

# Rule digest

1. Board only while a train is preparing: banner and left-column train icon; if neither shows, close partial, no panning (run-20261107-2140, run-20261110-0946-p1).
2. Standing rule, 2026-10-25 (see Errata): carriage 2, ticket 3 Trade Contracts (how-it-is-p1).
3. Other contract spending: record a reason, never diamonds (see Errata). Never summon a train or take the conductor role (limits-section).
4. Verify the carriage before tapping its plus when possible; gift dialog does not reopen (how-it-is-p1, day-20261101).
5. Skip the cinematic with one tap, then observe; no blind double-tap (how-it-is-p1, run-20261110-0413-p2).
6. Boarding closes about 5 minutes before departure; banner under about 2 minutes, tap carriage 2 first, read after (how-it-is-p2, run-20261111-0209-p1).
7. After riding, claim the "Train arrival reward" Event mail (how-it-is-p2, day-20261025).
8. Up to 1 regular and 2 Mega Express trains daily; two boardings in a day happened (cadence-section, open-questions-p1).
9. Verify the screen a tap opens; counters seen in passing are not that activity's data (run-20261109-2133, run-20261103-0035-p2).

Unverified: ticket benefit, reward split, Train Rating (open-questions-p1); tap points, pan routes, icon timing: old ticker units, not the current computer-use bridge (how-to-enter-p1, open-questions-p2).

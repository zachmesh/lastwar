# Secret missions

## What this activity is

Dispatching the Secret Command Post tasks and assisting allies with
theirs. This folder accumulates secret mission knowledge - task types,
durations, rewards and the assist cap. Executions are recorded in
`_records_/activity-executions/secret-missions.md`.

## How it is done

- Open the Secret Command Post truck on the base screen.
- Dispatch every own task, noting its name and duration.
- Assist allies until the daily assist cap is reached.
- Plunder Others is PvP. It is not touched without the owner explicit
  instruction, under the recording rules.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- Eight own tasks were dispatched, including Rescue Officer, Rescue
  Missing Agent, Search for Missing Refugees, Protect Refugee Camp, Train
  Refugees, Retrieve Valuable Supplies and Retrieve Agent Profile.
- Durations shown ran from 1 to 2 hours.
- Five ally assists were used, reaching the daily assist cap as displayed.
- Assist rewards seen included a Mystery Supply Box, a Refined Iron
  Module, Upgrade Ore and Hero EXP.

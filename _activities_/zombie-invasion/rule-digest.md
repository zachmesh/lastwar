# Rule digest

1. Seed a unit only when a Zombie Invasion tab is seen live in the Events hub; the event ended 2026-11-14 (run-20261114-1833, day-20261028-p12).
2. While live, play as much as possible: no daily, session or hour cap; pause one hour only when no zombies are nearby (cadence-section-p6, cadence-section-p7).
3. Record exact coin_before and coin_after every unit (cadence-section-p1).
4. Dispatch squads in parallel (march cap 4), the second at once; abandon a failed batch for single steps (cadence-section-p1, cadence-section-p3).
5. Collect coins from the LOOT panel behind the gift-box icon with the plain blue Claim All; claim each row before the next parallel kill (cadence-section-p6, day-20261111-retro).
6. Open owned Coin Chests and redeem medals for Coin Chests; never diamonds or money (cadence-section-p5).
7. Stamina: Free Claim, then the 10 and 50 items; open the inventory items and record their count before calling stamina exhausted (scope-and-availability-p1).
8. Red lines: no diamonds or money, price tags, Renew, Finish Now, Legendary Claim All, assembling or teleporting (scope-and-availability-p1).
9. Never press Return or Esc; either quits the game (day-20261028-p5).
10. Re-ground every map tap; a cleared area answers Search not found until the next wave (cadence-section-p2, scope-and-availability-p1).

Unverified: window-state and maintenance-dialog methods, measured by ticker units, not the current bridge (how-to-enter-p2, scope-and-availability-p2).

# Troop training finished batch lesson

## The rule

Press the pistol once per barracks per read whenever room could exist: the press classifies it [M]. A TRAIN UNITS panel means an empty queue; the toast "Drill Ground Full" means a finished batch waits (size from the tag or the digits); neither is "idle". A collect admits what fits and leaves the rest waiting [M]. Ready bubbles are not a clean mark; read digits from a crop of the SAVED frame.

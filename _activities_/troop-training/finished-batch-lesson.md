# Troop training finished batch lesson

Dated 2026-11-24, from the Coach's audit, evidence (`_records_/2026-11-22/coach/1215-partial-collect-evidence.md`) and control (`_records_/2026-11-22/troop-training/1454-queue-run.md`).

## The rule

Press the pistol once per barracks per read whenever room could exist: the press classifies it [D]. A TRAIN UNITS panel means an empty queue; the toast "Drill Ground Full" means a finished batch waits (size from the tag or the digits); neither is "idle". A collect admits what fits and leaves the rest waiting [D]. The ready bubbles are not a clean mark: read digits from a crop of the SAVED frame, not a zoom.

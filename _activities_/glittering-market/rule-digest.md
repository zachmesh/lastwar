2. Record the mechanism in the same pass: what it sells or gives, currency, daily free claim, refresh rule and cost, exchange list (the-job, open-questions).

5. The mail "Glittering Market Unclaimed" is never participation; a day with only that mail is reported "market not worked" (the-job, what-this-activity).
6. The 2026-11-02 search found no entry: base-screen icons, Events tabs and Mall tabs all negative; Gold Brick Mall sells paid packs and is not the market; nothing claimed (run-20261102-0508).

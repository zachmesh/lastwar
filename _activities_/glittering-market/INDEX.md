# Glittering Market

## What this activity is

A market the game calls Glittering Market. This folder
accumulates its knowledge. Executions are recorded in
`_records_/<date>/glittering-market/`, one file per execution.

The owner named it on 2026-10-31, in the same pass that ordered
the miss fixes, with the standing direction to collect from it
daily and maximize. It is modelled here the same day, as an
unmodelled item of the same class as crystal and daily missions
- known to the records only by its residue, never worked itself.

## What is known

- The market has never been opened, located or worked. Its only
  trace in the records is a mail titled Glittering Market
  Unclaimed, seen in the Event category on 2026-10-27,
  2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30, whose unclaimed rewards were taken
  by Event Claim All inside mail units
  (`_records_/2026-10-27/mail-rewards/2230-segment-2-run.md`,
  `_records_/2026-10-29/mail-rewards/2247-queue-run.md`,
  `_records_/2026-10-30/mail-rewards/1937-queue-run.md` and
  `_records_/2026-10-30/mail-rewards/2313-queue-run.md`).
- That mail is unclaimed residue. Whatever the market offers
  while it runs - daily free items, refreshes, exchanges - was
  never taken at the source on any recorded day, and its entry
  point, currency and rules are all unrecorded.

## The job

1. Locate the entry. Candidates in order - the Events screen and
   its tabs, the Store and its sections, then any base-screen
   icon carrying the market name. Record where the entry is,
   with the screen it was reached from.
2. Record the mechanism in this file in the same pass - what the
   market sells or gives, its currency, the daily free claim if
   any, the refresh rule and cost, and the exchange list worth
   taking under the standing red lines (no spending of diamonds,
   rubies or money without owner approval).
3. Work it daily from then on - every claimable or exchangeable
   item of the day processed, and the day closed on a live read
   of the market itself.
4. The mail titled Glittering Market Unclaimed never counts as
   participation. It is residue of a market not worked, and a
   day whose only glittering record is that mail is reported as
   market not worked.

## Cadence

- Until located - attempted or reported by name under rule R5 of
  the task unit queue design, as an owner-named item.
- Once located - daily, per the mechanism the locating unit
  records here, and a standing line on the daily collection
  ledger.

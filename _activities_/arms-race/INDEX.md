# Arms race

## What this activity is

The solo Arms Race event. Themed phases rotate through the day, and
each phase pays points for matching actions - training troops in the
Unit Progression phase, for example - toward point chests and a phase
ranking. The owner standing instruction of 2026-10-25 is to check
every four hours, aligned with the phase rotation, and complete the
tasks that can be completed under the standing limits below. The
owner standing rule of 2026-10-26 raises the bar - every four hour
run shall meet the Arms Race target of the phase live during the
run, meaning points reach the phase top chest threshold and every
earned chest is claimed. How the target is pursued, and what
happens when it cannot be reached inside the limits, is set out
under Cadence below.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/<date>/arms-race/`, one file per execution.

## How to enter

- Open Events from the base screen.
- Open the Arms Race banner. The current phase, its tasks and the
  time left in the phase are shown on the event page.

## Cadence

- Phases rotate every four hours, six phases per day - confirmed by
  the owner check cadence and the phase timer seen in game.
- Standing check - every four hours, complete what can be completed
  in the current phase and claim any chests already earned.
- Standing target rule, owner 2026-10-26 - every four hour run
  shall meet the Arms Race target of the live phase. The target is
  the phase top (third) chest threshold - 75,000 in Unit
  Progression, 30,000 in Base Building, read live for other phases.
  The run works toward it from the start - read the phase and its
  scoring table first, do the regulars that score in this phase
  inside the phase window (training queues, radar and doom work
  where it scores, harvests and collections, completions of
  buildings or research already under way, donations), re-read the
  points before closing the run, and claim every chest earned.
- Collect the reward, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - reaching the
  target includes getting paid. Claim every earned point chest in
  the same visit, and claim the phase ranking reward chest
  whenever it becomes claimable - if it lands after the phase
  rolls, the next run checks for it and claims it then. A reached
  target with an unclaimed reward counts as unfinished.
- Completion sweep, owner correction 2026-10-26 - the three
  point chests are only one reward set, not the definition of all
  Arms Race rewards. Before Arms Race is marked complete, return
  to the Events screen and scan the Arms Race card and page again,
  including its ranking and reward areas and the cumulative
  counter chest at the top left of the phase page above the
  point track. Treat an animated or
  bouncing chest, or a red dot attached to a reward, as an
  unclaimed reward until it has been opened and inspected. Claim
  every additional earned reward found. Arms Race is complete
  only when no bouncing chest or reward red dot remains, or when
  the screen itself proves a visible chest is still locked and
  its unlock condition and time are recorded.
- Score check every run, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - every
  four hour run reads the live Arms Race phase and score (points
  against the chest thresholds) and records the reading in the
  run record, whether or not the phase is that run focus. When
  the run is reported in chat, the current Arms Race score is
  stated with it.
- Just enough effort, owner refinement 2026-10-26 - the effort is
  calibrated to reach the target and no further. Once the points
  reach the top threshold, stop doing extra scoring work for that
  phase - no additional queues, claims or actions taken only to
  push the score higher. Overshooting wastes actions and
  resources that score better in a later phase or on a duel day,
  so plan the scoring actions to land at or just past the
  threshold.
- Hero Advancement phase, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - this
  phase is met by upgrading hero levels with EXP points. It is
  the easiest phase to close - spend hero EXP one level at a
  time, only until the target is met, then stop. This is a scoped
  authorization to spend hero EXP for this phase only. It does
  not extend to skill medals or hero shards, and it does not
  change the Alliance Duel Day 4 gate, where hero resources
  remain the owner spend. If the EXP on hand is short, open
  experience boxes from the inventory to top it up (owner
  instruction, 2026-10-26) - open only what the level ups
  actually need, in line with the just enough rule.
- The target never overrides the red lines. Diamonds and money are
  never spent, speedup and stamina items are never burned, no
  research or building is started on the agent initiative, hero
  resources are never spent, and stages are never swapped - not
  even to reach the target. If the top threshold cannot be reached
  inside those limits, earn what is reachable, claim what is
  earned, and record the shortfall as a missed target in the run
  record and in the chat report.

## Scope and availability

- Completing tasks with actions the account is doing anyway, or with
  free actions, is in scope.
- Burning speedups, stamina items or resources purely to score, and
  anything costing diamonds or money, stays out of scope without
  owner approval, under the recording rules.
- Observed on 2026-10-25 - the Unit Progression phase was live with
  this account at 0 points, rank 2110 as displayed, about three and
  a half hours left in the phase at the time of the first daily round.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- The Unit Progression phase pays per action as listed - training a
  Lv.10 unit 31 points, using a 1 minute training speedup 10 points,
  buying a pack containing diamonds 30 points.
- Training scores when the batch is queued, not when it finishes -
  the 12:20 barracks queues of the intake round put this account at
  75,047 points and rank 3 in the Lv.29 to 30 contest segment when
  checked at about 14:02, with some 57 minutes left in the phase.
- Three point chests per phase, thresholds 15,000, 30,000 and
  75,000, tagged with ruby values 2,360, 4,800 and 11,800. Tapping a
  chest claims it; tapping again shows its contents. All three were
  claimed in the 14:02 check - contents itemised in the execution
  record. Higher chests pay SR resource chests, Skill Medals, Arms
  Race Badges and 5 minute speedups.
- A phase ranking reward chest sits on the ranking row; when and how
  it pays out is unconfirmed.
- The Base Building phase pays 1 point per Building Power point
  gained, 10 points per 1 minute Construction Speed-up used, and
  30 points per diamond in bought packs. Its chest thresholds are
  6,250, 12,500 and 30,000 - lower than the Unit Progression
  thresholds - so thresholds do change by phase, while the chest
  ruby tags (2,360, 4,800, 11,800) read the same. This account held
  0 points in the evening Base Building check (rank 7 in segment as
  displayed, rank 2110 in the combined list).
- Stage Swap: the Calendar button on the Arms Race page carries a
  red dot and opens a Stage Swap explanation. A stage can be swapped
  for another at the listed times (00:00 and 12:00 as displayed);
  chest status, point requirements and points earned all swap with
  it, and earning restarts in the new stage. Swapping is a strategic
  decision - never done autonomously.
- On 2026-10-25 evening the owner widened the standing four-hourly
  check from Arms Race only to all the regulars (see
  `_activities_/daily-activities` and `tools/regulars.json`); Arms
  Race stays on that list as one regular among the rest.

## What is known from 2026-10-26

- The Hero Advancement phase ran on the morning of 2026-10-26
  with a top chest threshold of 12,000 points. The 04:35 run
  scored 0 - at the time, its only scoring paths (hero resources
  or paid packs) were both treated as red lines, so the target
  was missed and reported. The owner then authorized the hero
  EXP path for this phase (see Cadence) and called it the
  easiest phase to close - one hero level upgraded with EXP
  points is the intended unit of effort.

- The Hero Advancement scoring and claim were verified end to
  end on 2026-10-26 - the displayed rate is plus 1 point per
  2,000 Hero EXP used at a time, so the 0456 one-level spend
  of 75.0M EXP read back at the 0505 recheck as exactly
  37,500 points (segment rank 8, combined 2110), clearing the
  12,000 top chest on its own. One level at the Lv.120 band
  therefore overshoots this phase by design of its cost; the
  just-enough rule is satisfied by taking exactly one level
  and no more. One tap on an earned chest claimed all three
  earned chests at once (combined Skill Medals 1.6K, Arms
  Race Badges, SR coin, food and iron chests and 68 of the
  5m speedups), the page chest counter moved 0 of 2 to 6 of
  2, and re-tapping a claimed chest shows only its contents.
  The ranking row chest opened only a contents preview while
  the phase was live, so the phase ranking reward is claimed
  after the phase ends, in the next run, as the owner rule
  already provides.

- The cumulative counter chest, found 2026-10-26 in the 0529
  run - a separate chest icon sits at the top left of the
  phase page, above the point track, with a counter and a
  progress bar. It accumulates across tiers: at the 0505 claim
  the page counter moved 0 of 2 to 6 of 2, and the chest then
  stood full and claimable (the owner saw it bouncing, and the
  Arms Race tab carried a red dot) even though all three point
  chests were already claimed. Checking only the point track
  is therefore not enough - this counter chest must be checked
  and claimed in every run, alongside the ranking row chest.
  One tap in the 0529 run claimed it, paying a gold ticket
  item 1, pink puzzle-piece items 2 and a red gem tagged 50;
  the chest then turned purple and read 6 of 8 with the bar
  about three quarters full, so progress carries past a
  claimed tier into the next tier's threshold. What action
  increments the counter, and the exact item names, are still
  unverified. The ranking reward chest preview was also read
  in full this run - Iron Chest (SR) 3, Food Chest (SR) 3,
  Coin Chest (SR) 3 for this account at segment rank 8.
- Owner correction later on 2026-10-26 - after the three point
  chests had been claimed, a bouncing reward chest was still
  visible on the Events or Arms Race screen and the owner stated
  that more rewards remained. The lesson is now part of the
  Cadence completion sweep above - three claimed point chests do
  not by themselves prove that every Arms Race reward has been
  claimed.

- The Tech Research phase, read live on 2026-10-26 - chest
  thresholds 6,250, 12,500 and 30,000 with the same ruby tags
  (2,360, 4,800, 11,800). Scoring as displayed - Increase Tech
  Power by 1pt pays 1 point, Use 1-min Research Speed-up pays

  no completion could land in the phase, and the remaining

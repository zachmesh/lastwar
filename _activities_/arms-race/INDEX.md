# Arms race

## What this activity is

The solo Arms Race event. Themed phases rotate through the day, and
each phase pays points for matching actions - training troops in the
Unit Progression phase, for example - toward point chests and a phase
ranking. The owner standing instruction of 2026-10-25 is to check
every four hours, aligned with the phase rotation, and complete the
tasks that can be completed under the standing limits below. The
owner standing rule of 2026-10-26 raises the bar - every four hour
run shall meet the Arms Race target of the phase live during the
run, meaning points reach the phase top chest threshold and every
earned chest is claimed. How the target is pursued, and what
happens when it cannot be reached inside the limits, is set out
under Cadence below.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/<date>/arms-race/`, one file per execution.

## How to enter

- Open Events from the base screen.
- Events entry is layout-scoped (2026-11-09 0949): the (672,132) grounding was measured in landscape fullscreen and does not apply to letterboxed portrait fullscreen or windowed portrait - in both, that point is map scenery. Ground the Events entry fresh from the live layout each unit; first check whether the announcement banner hides the globe icon.
- Open the Arms Race banner. The current phase, its tasks and the
  time left in the phase are shown on the event page.

## Cadence

- Phases rotate every four hours, six phases per day - confirmed by
  the owner check cadence and the phase timer seen in game.
- Standing check - every four hours, complete what can be completed
  in the current phase and claim any chests already earned.
- Standing target rule, owner 2026-10-26 - every four hour run
  shall meet the Arms Race target of the live phase. The target is
  the phase top (third) chest threshold - 75,000 in Unit
  Progression, 30,000 in Base Building, read live for other phases.
  The run works toward it from the start - read the phase and its
  scoring table first, do the regulars that score in this phase
  inside the phase window (training queues, radar and doom work
  where it scores, harvests and collections, completions of
  buildings or research already under way, donations), re-read the
  points before closing the run, and claim every chest earned.
- Collect the reward, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - reaching the
  target includes getting paid. Claim every earned point chest in
  the same visit, and claim the phase ranking reward chest
  whenever it becomes claimable - if it lands after the phase
  rolls, the next run checks for it and claims it then. A reached
  target with an unclaimed reward counts as unfinished.
- Completion sweep, owner correction 2026-10-26 - the three
  point chests are only one reward set, not the definition of all
  Arms Race rewards. Before Arms Race is marked complete, return
  to the Events screen and scan the Arms Race card and page again,
  including its ranking and reward areas and the cumulative
  counter chest at the top left of the phase page above the
  point track. Treat an animated or
  bouncing chest, or a red dot attached to a reward, as an
  unclaimed reward until it has been opened and inspected. Claim
  every additional earned reward found. Arms Race is complete
  only when no bouncing chest or reward red dot remains, or when
  the screen itself proves a visible chest is still locked and
  its unlock condition and time are recorded.
- Score check every run, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - every
  four hour run reads the live Arms Race phase and score (points
  against the chest thresholds) and records the reading in the
  run record, whether or not the phase is that run focus. When
  the run is reported in chat, the current Arms Race score is
  stated with it.
- Just enough effort, owner refinement 2026-10-26 - the effort is
  calibrated to reach the target and no further. Once the points
  reach the top threshold, stop doing extra scoring work for that
  phase - no additional queues, claims or actions taken only to
  push the score higher. Overshooting wastes actions and
  resources that score better in a later phase or on a duel day,
  so plan the scoring actions to land at or just past the
  threshold.
- Hero Advancement phase, owner instruction 2026-10-26 - this
  phase is met by upgrading hero levels with EXP points. It is
  the easiest phase to close - spend hero EXP one level at a
  time, only until the target is met, then stop. This is a scoped
  authorization to spend hero EXP for this phase only. It does
  not extend to skill medals or hero shards, and it does not
  change the Alliance Duel Day 4 gate, where hero resources
  remain the owner spend. If the EXP on hand is short, open
  experience boxes from the inventory to top it up (owner
  instruction, 2026-10-26) - open only what the level ups
  actually need, in line with the just enough rule.
- Stamina refill, owner instruction 2026-10-28 (given via
  Slack) - when a phase scoring path runs on stamina (radar and
  doom work, for example), stamina may be refilled from the Free
  Claim or from stamina resource items (the 10 stamina and 50
  stamina items) so the scoring actions can be completed and
  the rewards claimed fully. Take the Free Claim first, then
  open only the stamina items the remaining gap needs, in line
  with the just enough rule. A target is not recorded as missed
  for lack of stamina until these refills have been used. This
  is a scoped authorization for stamina items only - it does
  not extend to speedup items.
- The target never overrides the red lines. Diamonds and money are
  never spent and speedup items are never burned; stamina items
  are used only under the stamina refill rule above. No
  research or building is started on the agent initiative, hero
  resources are never spent, and stages are never swapped - not
  even to reach the target. If the top threshold cannot be reached
  inside those limits, earn what is reachable, claim what is
  earned, and record the shortfall as a missed target in the run
  record and in the chat report.

## Scope and availability

- Completing tasks with actions the account is doing anyway, or with
  free actions, is in scope.
- Burning speedups or resources purely to score, and
  anything costing diamonds or money, stays out of scope without
  owner approval, under the recording rules. Stamina items are
  governed by the stamina refill rule under Cadence (owner
  instruction, 2026-10-28).

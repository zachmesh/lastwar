# Arms race

## What this activity is

The solo Arms Race event. Themed phases rotate through the day, and
each phase pays points for matching actions - training troops in the
Unit Progression phase, for example - toward point chests and a phase
ranking. The owner standing instruction of 2026-10-25 is to check
every four hours, aligned with the phase rotation, and complete the
tasks that can be completed under the standing limits below.
Executions are recorded in `_records_/<date>/arms-race/`, one file per execution.

## How to enter

- Open Events from the base screen.
- Open the Arms Race banner. The current phase, its tasks and the
  time left in the phase are shown on the event page.

## Cadence

- Phases rotate every four hours, six phases per day - confirmed by
  the owner check cadence and the phase timer seen in game.
- Standing check - every four hours, complete what can be completed
  in the current phase and claim any chests already earned.

## Scope and availability

- Completing tasks with actions the account is doing anyway, or with
  free actions, is in scope.
- Burning speedups, stamina items or resources purely to score, and
  anything costing diamonds or money, stays out of scope without
  owner approval, under the recording rules.
- Observed on 2026-10-25 - the Unit Progression phase was live with
  this account at 0 points, rank 2110 as displayed, about three and
  a half hours left in the phase at the time of the first daily round.

## What is known from 2026-10-25

- The Unit Progression phase pays per action as listed - training a
  Lv.10 unit 31 points, using a 1 minute training speedup 10 points,
  buying a pack containing diamonds 30 points.
- Training scores when the batch is queued, not when it finishes -
  the 12:20 barracks queues of the intake round put this account at
  75,047 points and rank 3 in the Lv.29 to 30 contest segment when
  checked at about 14:02, with some 57 minutes left in the phase.
- Three point chests per phase, thresholds 15,000, 30,000 and
  75,000, tagged with ruby values 2,360, 4,800 and 11,800. Tapping a
  chest claims it; tapping again shows its contents. All three were
  claimed in the 14:02 check - contents itemised in the execution
  record. Higher chests pay SR resource chests, Skill Medals, Arms
  Race Badges and 5 minute speedups.
- A phase ranking reward chest sits on the ranking row; when and how
  it pays out is unconfirmed.

## What was built (issue #854)

New `tapcal.py close` subcommand (1.5.0 -> 1.6.0): one
step of the panel-close ladder for a panel-close target:

  tap -> double-tap -> long-press -> click-element -> relaunch

- A tap sent while another surface is selected is named
  wrong-surface: it passes through to the base view and
  spends no method budget, and the next step is the same
  method after activating the covering surface. This is
  ticker-1704's first three attempts (127196 selected,
  panel on 127250), which `verify` would have scored as
  misses.
- Each method gets R2's budget of two no-change taps on
  the correct surface; two ignored on-surface taps spend
  the method and escalate to the next one.
- click-element is in the ladder only when the case names
  ocr_number - the issue makes it conditional on the X
  ever appearing in the catalog; without one it is skipped
  (long-press spent goes straight to relaunch).
- A relaunch places no tap (aim carries the X point,
  tapped null); a relaunch the panel survives counts as
  one spent attempt of its budget, not an unverified
  non-measurement.
- Every method spent ends exhausted: the R2
  process-failure report, not a fourth identical attempt.
- _tools_/INDEX.md tapcal row extended with the close
  ladder. No _logs_ appends on the PR branch.

# Amendment: close on the grid

**The inconsistency was mine.** Ruling 1 required "a witness that can move rose 0.1K"; ruling 2 treated the witness as corroboration only. At Quick Construction the Tech EXP bar cannot move and pass 001 did not read the badge, so its ninth tap met ruling 2 but not ruling 1. The coach is right.

**Fix: the grid is a better witness.** Rule 8 now fixes the refill clock. A grey timer whose end (read time plus timer) lies on the 20-minute grid proves the timer is the refill countdown of a spent pool. The check is free on every pass, needs no calibration tap, and by chance would match 0.08 percent of the time. A 0.1K rise is weaker, and unavailable where the bar is blind.

**Digest rule 6 and card R2, amended.** "A count figure needs a live read in the same unit. After the last tap the count is hidden by design; close as count spent when the live count before that tap was 1/30, the Donate turned from green to grey with a timer, and the timer end lies on the 20-minute refill grid within 3 s. A rise of the badge item or Tech EXP by 0.1K corroborates and is read when it is in the same frame; it is not required, since the 0.1M EXP bar at Quick Construction cannot move. Write the timer, its read time and the UTC time of the last tap. If the end is off the grid or unread, close unverified unless a witness rose. Never write a count after the last tap, derive one by arithmetic, or take one from the footer."

**Digest rule 4, one clause added.** After "the tap landed when the green Donate turned grey with a timer": "the timer end on the refill grid within 3 s confirms it".

**Strike** the sentence "An entry at 1/30 has no calibration tap: close it flagged uncalibrated" from the closing standard; the grid check needs none.

**Pass 001** closes DONE under the amended rule: timer end 17:57:57 within 0.3 s of the grid.

**Limit.** An off-grid end means a regime change or a misread: re-anchor on it and close that pass on a witness or unverified.

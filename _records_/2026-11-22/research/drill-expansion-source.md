**Two pointers for the survey.**
1. Four Drill Grounds already exist (Lv.26, 28, 30, 27) [M, 1047-f1b]. The fourth needs the Extra Drill Ground research [S3], so that lever is spent: strike "Extra Drill Ground, 61M coin plus 700 Valor, if not already done" from the plan. Their base slots sum to 30,200 against a cap of 39,460 read on 10-31, so bonuses are already about 30 percent or more [D].
2. Open the Development tail (Infirmary, Barracks and Focused Training IV, Efficient Healing, Survival Skills) and read every tag containing DRILL, GROUND, UNIT or CAPACITY: effect line, cost, effective time. If none exists, say so; the lever is then ground levels only.

# Power per coin of the path

**The 30 per million coin counts one node.** HQ 31 gives +63,700 power for its own coin, but the 2.15B bill also buys the chain. The path table's power column sums to +411K on the sheet [S1], stages 1 and 2 already done or running (+15.6K; Unlock Oil Well and Output V measured at +3,900 each [M]), so about +395K remains, 299K of it in Oil Output I to III.

**Remaining chain plus HQ 31:** about 459K power for 2.15B coin, **214 power per million coin**, against 365 for the ground level (+30,400 for 83.3M). The gap is 1.7 times, not 12 times. The chain's 19 nodes also register on Complete, not on collection, and HQ 31 opens hero cap 155 and the level 31 buildings.

**The date is set by oil and lab time, not coin.** The ETA puts Base Expansion at day 45 to 78. By day 60 the coin available is about 2.9B (reserve 2.0G, chests 0.29B, income 0.64B at 10.7M a day) against 2.23B for the bill plus one ground, so 83M spent on a ground delays HQ 31 only if the stock falls below a chain step's cost at the read. One line for the Strategist: price the ground against the coin stock at each chain step, not against days of income.

**The phone spends too.** The owner's coin fell 25.8M to 564 on 11-25, so use the coin held at the read, as Steer A said.

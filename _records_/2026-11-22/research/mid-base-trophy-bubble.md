## What can be claimed

The Arena is PvP. Daily milestones (1, 3, 5 challenges or wins) need fights;
ranking and alliance rewards settle at event end. The one no-fight reward is
the **Like** on podium players, 30 diamonds each for the top three, no battle
attempt used (three sites agree). Unconfirmed for this
account: the 2026-11-21 Arena screen demanded a top-200 Total Hero Power rank
and no Like button was described.

## Safe route

- Open: fresh base screenshot, tap the bubble. A radial menu offered VS, a cup
  and info on 2026-11-21. Use info or the cup only. Proven alternative: Events
  hub, Event Reminder, ARENA screen.
- Claim: thumbs-up under a podium player. A claim shows the button lit, the
  like count up one and diamonds up 30.
- Never press: VS, Challenge, Battle, Go, Join, Register, list refresh (costs
  diamonds after 3), lineup, shop, any dialog with a cost.
- Falsifier: a challenge list and no Like buttons means no claim. Record it, stop.

## Operator unit paragraph

Open the Arena from the mid-base trophy bubble (re-ground it on a fresh
screenshot). If a radial menu appears, press info or the cup, never VS. Read
the title, tab, timer and diamonds. Press each active thumbs-up Like on a
podium player, then read diamonds again. Press Claim only on an already-earned
Rewards row. Never press Challenge, Battle, VS, Go, Join, Register, refresh,
lineup, shop or any costed dialog. Stop at a challenge list, a confirmation,
or when nothing is claimable; close with the panel X. Record screens, diamonds
before and after, and the verdict. Cap 8 steps.

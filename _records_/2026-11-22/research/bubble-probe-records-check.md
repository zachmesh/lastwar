# Bubble probe: what the records hold

**1. NEW! bubble: probably the News Center, already known.** Scout seq 18 [M]: the small framed portrait at bottom-centre-right with the yellow NEW! bubble is the News Center entry; a tap at (700,870) opened it; the NEW! does not clear after one card. The panel holds news cards, not a claim, and its back arrow has a known blocker (unresponsive to a tap and to a long-press). If the bubble at (520,860) sits on that portrait, skip the tap; the glyph read is enough.

**2. Wrench bubbles: not in the records.** The only wrench recorded is a lower-left dock icon with a badge 1. A wrench over a building is unidentified, so this tap is new information.

**3. Question-mark figures: partly known.** The survivor-intake card says recruiting is free, **tap the figure, not the bubble**, confirm the "?" and the figure first, and read the recruit card; a cleared "?" or a smiley without a card is unverified. An earlier figure tap (11-04) opened no card. All of that concerns the gate line at the bottom edge; whether the five figures at the aircraft field are the same kind is unknown. The probe order says tap the bubble; the card says the figure: follow the card.

**Correction.** I had read the gold-trophy bubble near (568,217) as best fitting the Arena. Seq 17 identifies the Arena as the blue-roofed dome at (810,360), with its own NEW tag marking the unentered VS entry; the glowing orange-topped building at the top right carries a separate NEW tag and its identity is open. So the trophy bubble is not the Arena [M]; my 11-22 inference is withdrawn.

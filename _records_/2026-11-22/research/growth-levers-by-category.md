# Growth levers by category

## Bottom line

Guides give a stage order, not a power ranking. None publishes verified
power per unit; the one that tries calls its formulas "not fully publicly
verified" and stops at T5 troops, so it is GUESS. The panels give numbers.

## Measured per unit (TESTED, repository records)

| Lever | Power | Cost and time |
|---|---|---|
| Troop T8, T9, T10 | 1,063, 1,352, 1,647 each | T10 batch of 793 = 5.5M food + 5.5M iron, 1d 17h |
| Gold Mine to Lv.30 | +14,800 | 6M food, 6M iron, 1.9M coin, 6m24s; Lv.30 is probably the cap (inferred) |
| Drill Ground Lv.26 to 27 | +21,600 | next level 14d 7h |
| Builders Hut Lv.25 to 26 | +17,500 | 170M iron, 56.9M food, 7d 7h; needs HQ 26 |
| Research Defense Training VIII | +15,000 per completion | about 4.5d a level (3d left at 30 percent) |
| One hero level, 124 to 125 | +8,219 | about 83M Hero EXP |

Per food plus iron, a T10 troop returns about 0.12 power; the Hut about
0.00008. Stock is 1.1G iron, so queue-hours (builders, labs, barracks) bind,
not stock.

## Sourced order (SOURCED, shops and top-up sellers, spending advice ignored)

HQ and prerequisites, builders and labs never idle; research Development,
Economy, Garage 1, Alliance Duel, Special Forces; one squad type; troop tier
by waterfall; exclusive weapon to level 20 first (about +0.3M squad power per
milestone); Age of Oil route of 46 research levels. The hero cap is 155 at HQ 31 in the
repository and 175 in one guide; +5 a level reaches 175 at HQ 35, so they
probably agree. The real disagreement is when HQ 31 opens.

## Calendar

Base Expansion pays 30 duel points per building power point, top personal
chest 540,000 (TESTED, theme page): one Hut finish is about 525,000 points.
Finish long builds inside the Day 2 window, research on Age of Science,
troops on Total Mobilization. The owner rule bars speed-ups, so only
completion timing is controllable. Base Expansion is also the last Age of
Oil research; do not confuse them.

## Challenges to the model

1. Rank per queue-hour, not per resource.
2. Net power is noise: 145.1M to 150.2M swings, and three logged
   collections (+708K, +423K, +152K) exceed the +390K net. Keep a gross
   ledger and reconcile hospital and troop losses before ranking.
3. Core-squad-first is mostly moot: top heroes sit at the level cap.
4. HQ 30 is an inference; Unlock Oil Well research is already running.
5. Idle builders were a decision gap, not a cap: the Hut panel needs only
   HQ 26, and at 07:36Z all four builders were busy on Lv.25 Training Base
   upgrades after the owner decided on 2026-11-23.

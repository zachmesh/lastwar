# Harvest cap: what the guides say

**A cap exists, by one guide; no number.** The Higuma guide (Japanese, [S1]) says facility production must be collected before the cap: once the pile is full "production stops from the moment it is reached", and it deliberately gives no figure. The Last War Handbook guide and a plunder guide say nothing on pile caps. So "no cap" is unlikely; the size, and whether it is hours or an amount, is not in any source read.

**What the records hold.** The 09:19Z panel read shows Available figures below 4.50 h of output on all four resources, no "full" mark and no capacity line. The piles equal 3.34 h (coin), 3.30 h (food), 3.47 h (iron) and 3.65 h (oil) of the panel rate. Per-resource hours that differ are what a cap set by each building's level or storage would give, and what a fixed hour cap would not [G].

**What it changes.** The coach's three outcomes still hold, with the cap reading a little more likely: at a 3-hour wait a cap above 3.65 h reads 3.0 h of output; a rate effect reads 2.25 h; only a wait of 4 hours or more can show the cap itself.

**Cheap add, no unit.** The harvest unit can open one building's own panel once (read only, nothing pressed) and record any storage or capacity line; if the game shows one, the cap is a number, not a test.

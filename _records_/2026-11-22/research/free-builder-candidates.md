# Free builder candidates

**The records already price them.** Every building at Lv.24 or above is in the guide table, which matches four real panels: power gains exactly (Hospital +23,200, ground +30,400, Hut +17,500, HQ +63,700) and coin at 0.85 of the sheet (Hospital 68.85M against 68.8M read). A builders read is not needed to price; the unit that starts a building reads its preview anyway.

**Budget:** coin 14.8M plus typed chests 43.2M is 58M; 48M after the 10M floor.

| Building | Coin (M) | Power | Per M coin | Days at 2.325 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gear Factory 20 to 21 | 6.6 | +10,500 | 1,584 | 2.5 |
| Training Base 26 to 27 | 11.9 | +10,600 | 891 | 2.3 |
| Iron Mine 27 to 28 | 17.0 | +12,600 | 741 | 3.2 |
| Builder's Hut 25 to 26 | 18.7 | +17,500 | 936 | 6.6 |
| Farmland 28 to 29 | 23.8 | +14,900 | 626 | 4.2 |
| Gold Mine 28 to 29 | 23.8 | +14,800 | 622 | 4.2 |
| Smelter 26 to 27 | 35.7 | +15,700 | 440 | 4.6 |

All fit 48M. Over it: Barracks 24 to 25 (49.3M, fits 58M), Food Warehouse (58.6M), a ground (59.5M), Hospital (68.8M). Per builder-day the best are Training Base 4.6K, Gear Factory 4.2K, Iron Mine 3.9K; Hut 2.65K, ground and Hospital 2.3K, HQ 1.3K.

**Caveats.** Levels come from the 11-22 and 11-23 tag index; the game's Main Tasks rows (Gear Factory 21, Training Base 27, Hut 26, Hospital 26) agree. The Hut's 11-09 panel lists no coin row; the table says 22M on the sheet. Finishing on a Tuesday pays 30 duel points per building power point [TESTED].

**Not in the records:** the other Lv.20 buildings (Tavern, Emergency Center, Component Factory, Chip Lab, Material Workshop); the table starts at Lv.25. A guide page prices each, no game read.

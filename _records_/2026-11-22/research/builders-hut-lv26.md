# Builder's Hut Lv.26

**Answer: it does not add a builder.** Lv.26 gives +17,500 Power (90,800 to 108,300), free construction speed-up time 20 to 22 minutes, and a Hero EXP figure of 22.0M on the panel (reward or cost unread [G]).

**Evidence.**
- [M] The 2026-11-09 panel lists Power, Free Construction Speed-up Time, Hero EXP, costs and times, and no builder or queue line. Cost: Iron 170.0M, Food 56.9M, Headquarters Lv.26 met; 15d 08:05:18 original, 7d 07:16:49 effective.
- [S3] Three guides agree the Hut raises free construction speed-up time. The Last War Guide table has no builder column, and its Lv.25 to 26 row matches the panel exactly (90.8k to 108.3k, 15d 08:05:18, 20m to 22m).
- Builder count: the guides read give two queues (VIP 6 is the second builder). None documents a third or fourth, so how the account holds four, and whether a fifth exists, is unknown [G]. The Hut is not a source.

**Cost gap.** The guide lists gold 22.0M beside iron 200M; the panel's 15 percent reduction (iron 170.0M of 200M) makes it about 18.7M coin. The 11-09 read lists no coin row. Coin is scarce, so the next read must record every cost row [G].

**Value.** 17.5K power for about 7.3 builder-days is 2.4K per builder-day. The free-time gain is 2 minutes per upgrade start, about 0.02 percent of a seven-day build.

**Why the Main Tasks row exists.** Not shown; the guide's Headquarters table has no prerequisite columns. The HQ Lv.31 panel's prerequisite rows would say whether the Hut gates it.

**One lever.** Game8 says builder survivors sent to the Hut add free time [S1]; the slots are unread.

**The one read** (read-only, nothing pressed): the Hut Lv.26 panel at the next builders read: every cost row, whether Hero EXP is a reward, any queue line.

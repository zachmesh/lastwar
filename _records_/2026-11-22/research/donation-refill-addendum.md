# Donation refill addendum

**I withdraw 35 minutes as the favoured reading.** I re-ran the coach's numbers: 89 timers, 85 at 20.0 minutes or less, none between 20 and 35, four at 40.9 to 48.8, all 48 since 11-04 at 19.58 or less. A refill countdown tops out at its period, so the ceiling at 19.58 points to about 20 minutes. My 35 came from one gap and one count, which always yields a period.

**What H20 must still explain: the count, 8 against about 14.** Two causes are open [G]. Partial accrual (some ticks add nothing), or counts consumed outside our units: records note the owner also plays from a phone, and a phone donation uses the same count. A badge-item check cannot detect it: the badge rose 15.9K between the T1 close (82.9K) and the 1441 entry (98.8K) from other income.

**The decisive observation is the grid phase, not the count.** The first timer end after a pass lands on the refill grid whatever was consumed in between, so hidden taps cannot move it. The count only measures accrual.

| First timer ends (entry 17:45Z, last tap about 17:46Z) | Reading |
|---|---|
| 17:52:57, within 3 s | 35-minute grid; count 5 expected |
| 17:57:57, within 3 s | 20-minute grid; count 9 means clean, a lower count means counts consumed elsewhere or partial accrual |
| neither | no fixed grid |

The 20-minute grid points are :17:57, :37:57 and :57:57. At the 17:55Z entry the 20-minute end is 17:57:57, two minutes away, so read the timer within a minute of the last tap.

**Logging.** Entry time and count, every tap time, the first timer and its read time, to the second. No hand taps before the pass: a phone tap voids the count baseline but not the timer-end test.

**What would settle it.** One timer end on either grid, plus one more pass: if the period is 20, the second end sits on the same grid.

# Base Expansion ETA

Convention: the divisor is base time over effective time; research speed is divisor minus 1. Table in [the sidecar](base-expansion-eta.csv).

## Range

- **Base Expansion complete: 2026-12-31 to 2027-01-23, point about 2027-01-11** (day 37 to 60 from 11-24; the oil model gives 37 to 48 days for its 4.08M oil, lab time sets the later end) [G].
- **HQ 31 starts day 47 to 68 (2027-01-10 to 2027-01-31)** when its 1.44M more oil is held, and is **complete about 2027-01-25 to 2027-03-05** with the 52-day build cut by construction speed-ups [G].
- HQ 35 is not plausible: the chain needs 60.8M oil.

## Evidence

- **Research speed** [M, frame times]: Coin Output V at the owner's start +402 percent (divisor 5.02, upper bound); Food Output V +360 to +372 percent (4.60 to 4.72). A preview at 11:53Z gave +209 percent (3.095). Planning value for our own starts: 3.1 until one of ours reads higher.
- **Natural time** of the 879 base days on two labs: 175 days at 5.02, 186 to 191 at 4.6 to 4.72, 284 at 3.1.
- **Speed-ups:** at divisor 4.6 or more about half the 255-day stock reaches the oil date; at 3.1 about 94 percent.

## Assumptions

1. The other device used no speed-up item on either node (the stock read will show). 2. The guide's base time holds here (an idle node panel on 11-24 14:59Z confirms). 3. Research costs run below the guide (coin fell 4.6M against 6.2M, H103). 4. Oil follows the analyst's model: well, one 30K depot a day, More Oil Wells. 5. The second lab has no critical work until Oil Output I level 10.

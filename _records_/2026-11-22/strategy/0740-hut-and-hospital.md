# Builder's Hut and Hospital Lv.26

- **Builder's Hut is not a builder slot** [S1 game8.jp, Nov 2025]: it adds a free construction time reduction that grows with its level, and survivors dispatched to it (by rarity and rank) shorten builds further. Building slots are a separate item in the same guide. So the Hut cannot give a fifth builder; the Lv.26 row exists because the game expects the level.
- **The researcher's read** (record builders-hut-lv26, panel of 11-09 [M]): Lv.26 gives +17,500 power, free speed-up time 20 to 22 minutes, a Hero EXP figure of 22.0M (reward or cost unread), and no builder line. That free time is a threshold for a free finish, not the flat 6h32m (392 minutes), so the Hut's own level cannot be the flat (H134 mostly refuted); only dispatched survivors remain a candidate, and their slots are unread.
- **The cost of Lv.26** [D, guide rows and the panel]: base time 15.3 days (6.3 days real at factor 2.325 less the flat), iron 200M, another 67M row and gold about 18.7M, 2.4K to 2.8K power a builder-day against the ladder's 35.7K to 19.9K. The free-time step of 2 minutes a start is 0.02 percent of a seven-day build: nothing.
- **The Hospital is at Lv.25** (record 2357: 7,999 beds). The preview read at 04:53Z (building-queues/0453, coach) [M]: Lv.26 gives +23,200 power and +40 capacity for 68.8M coin: 337 power a million coin and 1.72M coin a slot, against the Drill Ground level's +30,400 power and +200 room for 83.3M (365 and 0.42M). My 300 to 400 beds [G] was about eight times too high, and +40 does not move the siege's room.
- **The risk it addresses:** after a repeat siege on 11-27 free room is 329, below the plunder guard of 1,000; a stop of about 4 days costs about 19M; collecting healed troops on new room frees beds at no cost, and a Hospital level does not.
- **One look, read-only, nothing pressed:** the Hut panel (free time, next-level value, survivor slots) and the Hospital's upgrade panel (cost, time, beds), in the 11-25 13:39Z hand-off read. Rows F-224 and F-225.

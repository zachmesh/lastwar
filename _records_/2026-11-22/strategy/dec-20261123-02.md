# Decision 02

- **Decision.** The three starts stand (Lv.26, Lv.27 and Lv.28 grounds, lowest first). Four amendments, no veto. Serves PL-08 and PL-14.
- **1. Food.** The previews must be read for the food line. The 11-10 panels listed food 170.0M and 314.5M for two of these levels, the 11-23 summaries listed iron only [M]. Three starts need about 978M food [D] against 648.5M held and a 100M floor: about 110 typed Food Chests UR (4.06M each, 169 held), converted 2 hours before (F-063).
- **2. Coin from fixed chests only.** Size the shortfall at run time from the HUD (need plus 10M floor minus coin on hand, plus 10 percent). UR then SSR then SR Coin Chests, one step each, quantity under 90. Do not spend plain Resource Chests on coin: 19,833 is 95 percent of the stock and each pays 10K food or iron but only 6K coin, so coin use forfeits 40 percent of face value while food is the scarce resource (gap near 2G over nine levels). RC1 showed no quantity cap, so the earlier cap risk is gone; the difference from the coordinator default is about 1.5 percent of the food programme and is not worth a veto.
- **3. Reserve released.** The 50M fixed Coin Chest reserve (R5) is released for these starts, by my decision. The backstop stays: 1.0G of UR and SSR choice chests unopened, 154M of SR choice chests unreserved. Coin income is 16M to 26M a day.
- **4. Fourth builder.** Training Base Lv.26 to 27 if its preview matches the guide (about +10.6K, 2.5 days, 11.9M coin): 4.2K a builder-day against 2.4K for the Hut, and it frees early. Else the Hut (936 power per million coin). The Barracks Lv.24 to 25 (F2: +19,500, 7.7 days, 49.3M coin) ties the Hut on builder-days but costs 2.6 times the coin per power. Never idle.
- **Steer A, 11:10Z (no veto).** Construction speed-up items first (about 167,760 minutes, building only); the general items (about 352K) stay as the flexible reserve. The three starts and their speed-ups happen inside the Tuesday window (until 2026-11-25T02:00Z), so the speed-ups also score duel points; the in-game Duel screen is the authority.
- **Unchanged.** Speed-ups 10,000 minutes each, 30,000 in all, after each preview; never Finish Now or the owner's queue; the owner may start first, so read the builders.
- **Alternatives.** Stagger the third start for coin: loses 19.6K a builder-day for days. Two starts and a Hut: loses 8 times the value per builder-day.

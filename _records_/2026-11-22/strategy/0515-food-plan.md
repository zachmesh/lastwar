# Food plan

## Needs, to about day 70 [D, sheet and panels]

| Item | Food |
|---|---|
| HQ 31 (at its start, day 47 to 68) | 1.30G |
| Base Expansion path (guide 618M; research cost factor 0.74 would give 457M) | 457M to 618M |
| Oil Well Lv.1 to 30 | 213M |
| Three ground levels at 09:20Z | 978M |
| Total | about 2.95G to 3.1G |

The other six ground levels (food about 3.0G) wait until HQ 31's food is secured.

## Supply [M and D]

Held 654M; income about 0.55G a month (19.6M a day) gives about 1.3G over 70 days; typed Food Chests UR 686M (169 at 4.06M). That is about 2.6G, a gap of 0.4G to 0.5G.

## Sources to close the gap, in order

1. SSR and SR Food Chests if they exist (F-071, unread): possibly 1G to 3G.
2. Plain Resource Chests as food: 10K each, 208M for all 20,862.
3. Choice chests as food: UR 4.06M, SSR 1.35M, SR 169K (pay 67 percent more than coin); about 125 UR chests close the gap.
4. Harvests and alliance help raise the income line; Food Output V adds 2.5 percent.
5. Truck plunder pays 3.8M food a UR truck but costs power (decision 04): not a food plan.

## Rules

Convert food 2 hours before a start, the shortfall only, typed chests first. HQ 31's food is bought the week before its start. A ground level after the first three starts only if food after the next node and the HQ 31 reserve stays above 100M.

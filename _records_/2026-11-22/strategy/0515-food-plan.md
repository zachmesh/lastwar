# Food plan for the long line

## Output measured [M, harvest 1330]

Food 674.9K an hour (16.2M a day), iron 622.7K (14.9M a day), coin 441.9K (10.6M a day). Held: food 654.8M, iron 1.067G, coin 529K.

## Needs to the HQ 31 start [D, panels and sheet]

HQ 31 1.30G, Base Expansion path about 556M (research cost factor 0.90 [M]), Oil Well 181M to 213M, three ground levels 978M: about 3.0G. Coin about 2.5G (path about 1.65G, HQ 31 433.5M, grounds 262M, well 116M to 137M); iron about 2.8G, covered by the typed iron stock.

## Food date: when held food plus supply meets 3.0G

| Supply | Days from 11-24 | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Output alone | 147 | 2027-04-20 |
| Plus typed Food Chests UR (686M) and plain chests (208M) | 91 | 2027-02-23 |

Output alone nearly meets the need by the lab date if the lab binds (divisor 2.2, day 146). If the chain runs faster (divisor 3.1 or more, day 45 to 60) food binds, and trucks and chests must supply the difference.

## Coin

Output 10.6M a day gives 0.64G in 60 days against 2.5G: the chests (fixed 273.7M left, choice chests as coin about 1.97G) and truck coin (about 2.4M a UR truck) are the source, not output. Choice chests cover both coin and food, so the first 60 days use nearly all of them.

## Sources, in order

1. Own truck dispatch: a UR truck settles about 3.8M food, 3.8M iron and 2.4M coin [S1, 11-09 and 11-10]; the trade screen has not opened since 11-11, so this source is unproven (F-126, F-127).
2. Typed Food Chests UR; SSR and SR Food Chests if they exist (F-071); plain chests as food (208M).
3. Choice chests as food (UR 4.06M, 67 percent more than as coin).
4. Harvest bubbles (the 1330 Collect gave 0.7M food), alliance help; Food Output V adds 2.5 percent.
5. Truck plunder: costs power (decision 04), not a food plan.

## Rules

Convert food 2 hours before a start, the shortfall only, typed chests first. HQ 31's food is bought the week before its start. A ground level after the first three starts only if food after the next node and the HQ 31 reserve stays above 100M.

## The test first

At least 20K a level moves the honor and star shard buys to rank 2; less keeps them on hold. It is held items, not a purchase; the planner may stage it before the Thursday application.

## Ranking

## Exit plan for every buy

Tap the item; confirm the panel names an earned currency icon and quantity; press Buy once and one confirm. If a diamond icon, a recharge or store page, a Go button or any other app appears, press only the in-game close or back (never Escape), answer nothing, stop and report. Black Market refresh: only when the label reads Free.

# Decision 01

- **Decision.** Lab 1 (11:43Z), lab 2 (11-26 07:19Z) and the four builders (11-24 09:20Z) go to training slots: the Extra Drill Ground path and its new ground first, then ground levels, with speed-ups under Steer A's bounds. Requeue only into measured free slots.

- **Rationale.** In Base troops are 40,235 and Full was read at 07:49Z and 08:00Z. A slot is worth +1,647 as a new T10 troop for about 6.9K food and iron, realizable once the slot exists. The hospital Collect has no selector and its toast names all 5,306, so it likely needs 5,306 slots at once (partial space untested, H16); one level probably collects none. The speed-up decision 2425 stands for ground levels because each level is +329,400 as new training, not because of the hospital.
- **Alternatives.**

  2. Builders on Training Base and Hut (4K to 5K a builder-day): lost to 15K to 34K a builder-day; they take the idle slots after.
  3. Wait for a 5,306-slot jump to collect the hospital: lost, it delays realized power for weeks and saves only 37M food and iron.
  4. Dismiss low tiers: deferred, irreversible, controls unseen (Q2).
- **Expected gain.** New ground at Lv.1: up to +3.29M by about 11-30 [D, S1]. Each ground level +0.35M [D]. Window 7 to 30 days. Slots arrive only when a level or ground completes, not now.
- **Cost.** Valor about 700 (unread), coin about 61M for the node, 59.5M and 119M for the first levels, food 20M, 221M and 442M, up to 10,000 speed-up minutes an upgrade, 35,000 a week. Binding: Valor and the lab path length; at most six levels remain on three grounds (HQ cap).
- **Evidence.** Slot value and +200 a level [M]; costs [D] (guide times 0.85, five exact matches); Extra Drill Ground [S1, two guides] unread here.
- **Guardrails.** No diamonds, no Finish Now, no speed-up on the owner's queue, floors held, no march, no dismissal; the three times rule amended by proposal.

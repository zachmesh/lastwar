# Resource policy v3 draft

Owner words relayed by Steer A: "clearly and carefully prioritize for growth", "make your own decision". Tags as in the seat duties.

## Floors (unchanged)

Food 100M, iron 150M, coin 10M, diamonds untouched, plus the duel-day reservation. Diamonds received (the 5,000 in each Mystery Supply Box) are never spent.

## The three times rule

Decision 2350 allows a start costing at most a third of each stock. Drill Ground L29 needs food 442M against 638M (69 percent) [D], so the rule would block the capacity program. **Proposal for the coordinator to ratify:** for ranked capacity sinks the test is stock minus cost at or above the floor. All other starts keep the rule.

## Coin

- Plan only on the 406M read (coin chests 281M, plain chests 125M). The 1.97B inferred from 2,662 Resource Choice Chests counts after one popup is read (PL-03).
- Conversions follow [box policy v1](0117-box-policy-v1.md): just in time, shortfall plus 10 percent, fixed Coin Chests first, choice chests last. Never hero shards, medals, ore or blueprints.
- Ranked sinks, best first (power per million coin): fourth ground and its levels (unread), Drill Ground L27 5.9K, L28 4.3K, L29 3.0K, L30 2.3K, Training Base about 1.0K, barracks and HQ about 0.15K. Age of Oil and HQ 31 stay off.

## Food and iron

Open Food Chest UR (169 at 4.06M) only when a food start is within 2 hours and food would fall below its floor. The six levels need 2.64G food, 874M iron [D].

## Speed-ups

Steer A bounds: preview first, no Finish Now, never the owner's queue, 35,000 minutes a week, log minutes and power. Decision 2425 default: at most 10,000 minutes per upgrade. Order: Extra Drill Ground path, then the cheapest Drill Ground level. Stock read 352,000 general, 168,000 construction, 15,000 or more tech minutes; scope of the last two is for Steer A to confirm.

## Earned currencies

Black Market cash, honor, alliance honor, campaign stars and crystals: spend on the best shards per currency from the preview, after the weapon and shard reads, and log power before and after. Never a diamond refresh, the Glittering Market, weekly passes, cash packs or anything PvP.

## Hospital

The 8.74M of healed troops is a ceiling, not a target. No resource is reserved for it; it probably needs 5,306 free slots at once (H16).

## Boxes

One box gave one unnamed chest tile (08:55:43Z), n = 1, distribution unknown, so the boxes are unranked. Steer A approved up to 22 in all, two batches, one at a time, stopping at a rare hit. Every Use resets the quantity to 1 and reads it first: the slider snaps back to the full count.

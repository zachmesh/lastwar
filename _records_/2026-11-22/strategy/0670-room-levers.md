## What the sources say

- **Ground levels** add +200 slots to Lv.30 and +100 a level from Lv.31 (the unit count table of lastwar-guide.org [S1]; our previews show +200 on two grounds [M]). Nine levels give 1,800 slots, so the ladder reaches at most the 1,519 barracks troops and not the hospital pile, which needs 5,306 at once if its Collect is all-or-nothing.
- **Hospital mechanics:** troops in a hospital do not count toward ground capacity [S1 ldshop.gg, M on 11-24: 1,000 wounded opened about 1,000 slots]. The guide says veterans cause losses on purpose; for us a swap of equal tiers is zero and of lower tiers a loss, so it is not a lever.
- **Promotion** of existing troops needs no new training; whether it needs room, and whether its panel opens, is unread [S1].
- **Extra Drill Ground research** is done; four grounds stand [S1].
- **No guide names** a capacity research node, alliance technology, hero or commander skill or item; the scouts' tree read found none [M].

# Lever ranking v3.0 draft

Rows with units and reads in [the sidecar](lever-ranking-v3.csv). Draft: the 12:30Z version replaces it after the capacity, research and store reads. Rank is by power per scarce unit, where the unit differs by lever, so read the column "scarce unit" before comparing.

## The merge in one paragraph

The two pillars are one chain. Capacity (slots) is the gate for troops; materials (shards, medals, earned currencies) feed the hero block, which is 44 percent of power. Capacity has the biggest single numbers but all of them wait on reads; the materials engine has free inputs and a deadline (Black Market about Dec 13, honor reset Dec 1).

## Top four, and what would reorder them

1. **Extra Drill Ground path.** Two guides, a curve that matches three panel values. 2,000 slots as new T10 is +3.29M; the hospital is not part of it. If the node is done or Valor is short, it falls to rank 4.
2. **Promotion, +2.75M.** If the panel opens only with a free slot, it moves behind rank 1 in time, not in value.
3. **Weapons and shards.** Rests on earned currencies and the Black Market. The boxes are unranked: one draw, a chest, no inference.
4. **Six Drill Ground levels.** Certain per level (+200 measured), small (+0.35M), bounded by the HQ.

## Forecast, 30 days (a range)

- Base, no unread lever works: +1.8M (1.2 percent). About 2.0M is the hero line's own pace (66K a day, partly other-device play) less troop-loss noise; the plan adds little here.
- With promotion: +4.5M. With the new ground at Lv.1 filled by new T10: +3.3M more. Upper bound near +7M (4.7 percent) [G]. v2.0's "2 to 5 percent plus 8.7M" is replaced by this range.

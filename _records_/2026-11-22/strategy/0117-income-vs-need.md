# Income items against need

Need to day 60: coin 2.9G, food 4.9G, iron 3.9G with the six ladder levels. Own trucks (coin 9.9M, food and iron 15.6M a day, haircut 10 to 20 percent) are 20 to 24 percent of each need and decide whether the ladder is funded. The Assist (1.6M iron and food a day) is 2 to 3 percent, a baseline term. The Coin pass chest (0.3M to 7.3M a day for 6 days, at most 1.5 percent) and the boxes (329, about 30M coin-equivalent, 1 percent, plus 0.16G if 30 a day last 60 days) do not move the plan: items received, not levers.

# Event store ranking rubric

Steer A allowed spending event tickets in the event store (rows priced in tickets only; never a diamond, gem or money row). I rank by value per ticket in the plan's terms, highest first:

1. **Oil.** Oil binds the path: 66.9K a day is one day of the oil gate for Base Expansion and HQ 31, so each 66.9K of oil brings the line one day sooner. No chest holds oil.
2. **Hero shards for heroes we use** (Lucius and Schuyler stars, the Murphy weapon test): about 2.5K to 8K power a shard [G, from 120 of 680 shards giving +0.3M to +1.0M]. Shards for heroes off our path are worth little.
3. **Food and coin** (the pair that binds the ladder): about 0.7K power a million through the ladder [G]. A plain resource row is worth that and no more.
4. **Chests** by the box policy: an SR choice chest is 169K food or 101K coin; a typed iron chest is not scarce.
5. **Speed-ups last:** the construction stock is 141K minutes and gains about 2.6K a day, the general stock 352K under the weekly cap, and oil, not time, binds: a speed-up adds almost nothing beyond the cap.
6. **Iron, decorations, medals, Drone Parts:** low; Drone Parts only if PL-05 shows a use.

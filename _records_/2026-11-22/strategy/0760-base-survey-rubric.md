# Base survey ranking rubric

The owner asked that the operator scroll the game's screen to see all areas and interact with them. Steer A's reading: the base and maps are larger than the window; a read-only survey comes first, then one interaction unit per ranked area under the existing rules. I rank each find by value per unit, with weights food 1.0, coin 1.67, iron 0.5 and a unit's cost about 0.3 USD. A find clears when it is worth about 100K resource-equivalent, a power gain, a duel row, or a stated information value.

1. **A free collect or claim** (a bubble, pickup, gift box, wandering survivor, a hut or building tag with a plain Claim): value is the items at the weights, read from the first claim; fold it into an existing regular (PL-19) if it recurs, since a new unit must name its value.
2. **A building not in our queue** (Hospital, Builder's Hut, Gear Factory, Training Base, Alliance Support Hub, wall, Tech Center): an upgrade is a ruling, not a claim. It is ranked by power per builder-day against the ladder's 20K to 36K: the Hut is 2.4K, the Hospital about 2K to 3K [G] (note 0740), so none beats the ladder unless its panel shows a lever (a speed bonus, a builder slot, free time).
3. **Decorations and placed objects:** decoration power was +7.7K a day [M]; a decoration that is free to place or level is a power gain: price it at 0.7K power a million.
4. **Survivors on the ground:** a recruit scores +4,500 on a duel day with a recruit row and adds a survivor; low unless the day's rows list it.
5. **Anything that sends troops, burns stamina or touches players** (a gather march, zombies, a commander): out, by the standing rules.
6. **The world map** is surveyed separately, read only; I rank resource tiles only against the exposure rule.

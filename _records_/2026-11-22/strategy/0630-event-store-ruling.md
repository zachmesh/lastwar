# Event store ruling

Three event tabs spend earned free currencies (tickets or ingredients); nothing has been read in them. The unit serves PL-06; this page is the ruling the operator spends under in a later unit, once the read gives names, prices, balances, limits and timers.

## Rank by item type, best first

1. **Construction speed-up items:** about 24 power per minute on the ground ladder and they cut the HQ 31 build; the construction pool is claimed, so every minute counts.
2. **Tech and general speed-up items:** they shorten the chain one for one until oil binds (the plan uses 80 to 85 percent of the stock).
3. **Oil:** the binding resource; a barrel is oil without waiting.
4. **Food, then iron, then coin chests:** food is the largest gap (0515); iron is short only without the typed stock.
5. **Decoration components** (the decor line moves about 7.7K a day) and **skill medals** for Thursday.
6. **Hero universal and weapon shards:** last, since 680 and 441 are held and the stock covers the gaps (0147).

Diamonds, cash and any payment surface are never used.

## Spending rule

Buy fixed-price items in the order above, within per-person limits, and spend currency that would lapse at the event end at least 2 hours before it. A currency with no ranked item to buy goes to the Lottery.

## Lottery ruling

Draw only with free event currency that has no ranked store use, one press a draw and read each result. Cap: 10 draws, then stop and report the prize table and what dropped; extend only if the table shows ranked items. Stop at once on any diamond price, Buy, Go or payment screen. Draws are random, so each is a forecast row (F-146 to F-148).

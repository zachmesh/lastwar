# Trade day rules, 11-26, 2544

Steer A, 22:15Z: stage the cycles by default; the Strategist may amend by 01:30Z. The steps are sheet 3110's.

## Dispatch

Five own trucks a day, each into an EMPTY slot (a slot whose truck is still out stays). Dispatch only before 22:00Z on 11-26; later, settle only. Refresh: SR or better is the target; floor 80; at most 10 a slot and 60 a day; read the price line first; any diamond or other price: STOP.

## Badge rule v2 and the cargo

BADGE FIRST. The left-column vehicle-front icon opens Intercity Trade ONLY WITH A BADGE (a red dot or a count): a BARE icon is the Alliance Train shortcut by glyph, and a tap only pans the camera to the station: no tap on a bare icon. A bare or absent icon after ONE WORLD then BASE is a VERIFIED NOTHING-TO-DO (a state_read line, 3170 DONE). At entry write the icon state, BOTH tab badges, the count x of 5 and each slot's state. The Your Truck tab badge counts the empty unlocked slots while the count is below 5. Read the cargo from the Your Truck tab.

## Order on a dot

Read the Hospital free room BEFORE the tram tap or AFTER the plunders. On entry read the Others' tab FIRST and plunder before any other screen; then the Your Truck tab. NEVER leave the trade screen between the entry and the Others' tab.

## Downgrade test

A sample for the Coach (n=2 so far; cycle G did not run it), until her table has five: on the FIRST truck of the unit that reaches SR (not SSR or UR), BEFORE Let's Go do ONE MORE contract refresh (never a diamond price) and record the quality before and after. If it fell below SR, refresh again until SR or better, within the 10 a slot, the day's 60 and the floor of 80.

## Plunder, up to four a day (OD-20261123-20)

Final text, Steer A 11:27Z: ANY truck on the Others' tab that meets ALL FOUR: (i) the guard's power under Squad 1 (26.10M read 11-26 09:51Z), as read in the frame; (ii) arrival MORE THAN 30 MINUTES, a truck arriving in under 30 is skipped; (iii) not an alliance mate; (iv) the Hospital free room at least 1,000 (read before the entry or after the plunders, a read within the hour holds; under 1,000: 'blocked by capacity'). Order by expected loot: UR, then SSR, then SR by cargo class. No tier filter and no stack filter: record each card's 500-badge stack and its frame colour as DATA. Up to 4 a day. Stop after a fight that wounds more than 50. Quick Plunder once, Battle once, per fight.

## The siege of 11-27T01:15Z

Read the free room BEFORE the first fight of the day. No plunder after 22:00Z on 11-26, so the room is not spent before the siege.

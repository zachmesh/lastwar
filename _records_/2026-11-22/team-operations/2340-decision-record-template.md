# Decision record template

One file per decision, named `dec-<date>-<nn>`, under the growth-plan decision log (home chosen by the scribe). Fields:

- **Decision:** what will be done, in one sentence.
- **Serves:** the plan item (the same text as the unit's `serves:` line).
- **Rationale:** why this one now, in two or three sentences.
- **Alternatives:** at least two, with why they lost.
- **Expected gain:** a number, its unit and the window (power, per day or total, by date).
- **Cost:** resources by type, builder or queue time, and the binding constraint.
- **Evidence label:** measured (panel or record path), sourced (URL) or guess.
- **Guardrail check:** no premium spend, no speed-up, no fight, the 3x rule; theme-day fit.
- **Review date:** when the actual gain is read and compared.
- **Outcome:** filled at review: actual gain, difference, lesson.
- **Status:** proposed, started, collected, reviewed.

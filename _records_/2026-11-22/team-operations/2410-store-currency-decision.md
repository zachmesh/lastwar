# Decision: free earned store currencies may be spent

## Owner delegation (relayed, 2026-11-23 08:19Z)

Spending FREE EARNED currencies in the stores is approved: Black Market cash, honor, alliance contribution, campaign stars and crystals, ranked by power or shards per currency from the in-game previews.

## Still forbidden

Diamonds, real money, the paid Black Market refresh, weekly passes, cash packs, the Glittering Market, and anything PvP. No purchase of any kind with a currency whose source is not free earning.

## Procedure

1. **Read first, buy later.** The scout's read-only units record each store: balance of each currency, every item with its CURRENCY, price and daily or total limit, the Black Market end date. Nothing is bought in those units.
2. **Rank.** The strategist and planner rank the items by power or shards per currency (the exclusive-weapon shard choice chest, UR decorations, Honor Store and Alliance Store universal items, the Campaign Store), recording the evidence tag of each estimate.
3. **Buy.** A unit buys only a ranked item with a free earned currency, and never an item priced in diamonds or real money; if a price or currency is unclear, stop and record the text. Each purchase logs the exact Total Power or shard count before and after in the ledger and says what it serves.
4. **Reserve.** Keep the currencies that a ranked later item needs; do not spend a balance on a lower-ranked item.
5. **Stale balances.** Re-read each balance in the same unit before buying.

## Effects

This adds a no-queue, no-coin lever: shard and decoration purchases do not need a builder or a lab, and they spend currencies that otherwise sit idle.

# Research rules

Part of [the sidecar](0054-operator-handover-detail.md). Tree route and the running node icon are also in [2700-routes.csv](../queue-planning/2700-routes.csv) (row age of oil tree).

- Both labs busy: the HQ preview (green arrow) row "Lv.1 Base Expansion" Research button opens THE AGE OF OIL tree. A lab idle: tap the running node's icon (456,925). Never the fast-forward (603,925).
- Read the Finish Now clock, not the Research button, as the effective time (research speed M 3.678). Speed-ups: tech items only, item name and quantity read before each press, one at a time; press Complete the moment a node finishes.
- Coin: N = ceil((cost + 10M - coin now) / 2.4364M) UR Coin Chests: tap the tile, read its name, number field and Total, one Use. Reserves: food 100M, iron 150M, coin 10M.

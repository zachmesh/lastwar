### OD-20261123-14 - what happens to the run articles that duplicate a record

- opened: 2026-11-23
- item: the splits left 1,767 `_activities_/<activity>/run-*.md` articles; the
  coach's audit (`_records_/2026-11-22/coach/0055-run-articles-audit.md`) finds
  1,108 duplicates of a record (class a, 393,089 words), 554 unique rule or
  lesson (class b, an upper bound) and 105 with no record (class c).
- context: per-run facts have two homes (the article and the dated record);
  reflection's type-instance-forms says two homes drift apart. Improvement
  IMP-20261123-13.
- status: DECIDED 2026-11-23T09:10Z by the owner, relayed by Steer B.
- decision: MARK, THEN MOVE TO A NEW LOCAL TYPE (not the mark-only option). The
  1,108 class a files get status superseded and a pointer to the record, then
  move; the 554 class b and 105 class c files stay in `_activities_` until a fold
  absorbs them into a topic article, then they are marked and moved the same way.
  Records are never edited; a moved-paths table resolves the old paths. Nothing
  moves before the move tool and the registered type exist. Type name: the
  coordinator names and registers it in typed-folder-adoption (proposal
  `_run_notes_`, one folder per activity).

### OD-20261123-15 - activity playbook cards that give take-aways and improve over time

- opened: 2026-11-23
- item: organize `_activities_` so each folder gives TAKE-AWAYS, not lists of
  files; name who takes the role; make the system improve over time.
- context: measured by Steer B (IMP-20261123-14): 31 rule digests hold 267
  rules and all are `status: unverified`; 332 of their citations point at run
  articles that are about to move, only 36 at records; INDEX pages are mostly
  link lists; unit records carry no note of which rule helped or failed.
- status: DECIDED 2026-11-23T09:25Z by the owner, relayed by Steer B.
- decision: each activity INDEX.md becomes a PLAYBOOK CARD of 300 words or fewer
  (goal; the verified route of at most 8 steps; the 5 rules that matter with
  status and date; the 3 commonest failures and fixes; the 3 open questions each
  with its test; a numbers line generated from the records; 5 links at most).
  Rules cite RECORDS, never run notes; a superseded rule is rewritten in place
  (history in git), no growing Errata sections. Roles, no new seat: scribe =
  playbook editor; coach = playbook quality owner (rotates one activity a day,
  names the 3 worst weekly); researcher = verifier (turns an unverified rule into
  a falsification test); planner stages it; operator = sensor (three new unit
  stats: rules_used, rule_failed, gap); coordinator = clock and dispatch; the
  owner decides policy conflicts. Weekly loop; an improvement closes only when
  its metric moves. P2, pilot first (alliance-donations and shop-claims), after
  the P1 landings.

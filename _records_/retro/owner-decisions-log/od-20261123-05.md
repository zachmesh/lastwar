### OD-20261123-05 - may the Mac take the game session over from another device

- opened: 2026-11-23
- item: whether the operator may tap Confirm on the game's "account is currently
  active on a different device" dialog, which takes the session over from the
  other device, and under what conditions.
- context: the vip unit of 2026-11-23 06:34Z found that dialog on its first
  frame; the operator tapped only the X and stopped (record
  `_records_/2026-11-22/team-operations/2240-account-elsewhere.md`). It is not
  known whether the owner was playing on another device. Waiting costs the
  2-hour Secret Missions claim (can be plundered if left unclaimed) and later
  claims; taking the session over could disconnect the owner mid-play.
- decision needed (owner): (1) is the owner playing elsewhere now; (2) a standing
  rule, for example "Confirm only when the owner has said they are off the other
  device" or "never Confirm, wait for the owner" or "Confirm when idle for N
  minutes"; (3) whether the operator checks for this dialog on every first frame
  (it already stops on it).
- status: ANSWERED 2026-11-23T06:47Z by the owner, relayed by the Steward.
- decision: (1) the owner approves tapping Confirm on that dialog, taking the
  session back and kicking the other device out; a STANDING approval, not a
  one-off. (2) Guard: if the dialog comes back within 30 minutes of a Confirm,
  someone is playing on the other device: do not Confirm again, pause desktop
  play for that window, record it as a normal result, retry after 30 minutes
  without asking. (3) The owner will not be asked about operational choices

### IMP-20261123-14 - activity folders hold take-aways and a loop that improves them

- source: owner instruction 2026-11-23 to Steer B: organize `_activities_` so it yields
  take-aways and not lists; name who owns it; make the system improve over time.
- priority: P2, pilot first.
- class: process
- leak: the 31 rule digests hold 267 rules, all `status: unverified`; 332 citations
  point at run articles about to move, 36 at records; INDEX pages are mostly link lists;
  unit records say nothing about which rule helped or failed.
- design: each `INDEX.md` becomes a playbook card of 300 words or fewer: goal, the
  verified route (8 steps), the 5 rules that matter with status and date, the 3
  commonest failures and fixes, the 3 open questions with the test that settles each,
  a numbers line generated from records, 5 links. Rules cite records, never run notes.
  A superseded rule is rewritten; history stays in git (C2).
- roles: the scribe edits cards and digests; the coach owns quality (daily rotation of
  one activity, the numbers, staleness, corrections); the researcher turns an unverified
  rule or open question into a falsification test the planner stages and the operator
  runs; the operator adds `rules_used`, `rule_failed` and `gap` to the unit stats. No
  new seat.
- loop, weekly: the coach names the 3 worst activities (lowest done rate, most
  `rule_failed`, oldest unverified); scribe rewrites, researcher tests, the next week
  measures again. An entry closes when its metric moves.
- tool: `playbook-check` (toolsmith) validates the card shape, that every cited path
  resolves, and flags rules unverified over 14 days.
- target metric: verified share of rules, 0 of 267 now; `rule_failed` per 100 units,
  baseline first; unverified over 14 days, baseline first.
- verification: two pilot cards (alliance-donations, shop-claims) pass `playbook-check`;
  the first weekly coach report names its 3.
- status: open - the coordinator dispatches the pilot.

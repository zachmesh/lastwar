### IMP-20261126-02 - free alliance help is requested on every build the team starts

- source: helmet tests 13:58:22Z (the 13-day ground) and 14:24:47.8Z (the Oil Well Lv.26 to 27);
  Steer A rulings 11:27Z and 14:04Z; coach file `help-evidence-h118.csv`.
- class: lever never used
- leak: the white round helmet bubble over an upgrading building is the Request Help control; the
  team's units never pressed it, so every build the team started ran without helps while the
  owner's phone requested them on its own builds. One press brings 30 of 30 helps from the alliance
  within about 13 to 22 minutes.
- root cause, MEASURED: per help the game takes the larger of 0.5 percent of the time left and a
  flat 21.567 minutes (it was 20.067 on 11-25: the crossover moved from 2.79 to 3.00 days); the
  13-day ground lost 46.0 h (k = 5, 7, 9, 13, 30 at the reads), the Oil Well 10 h 47 min.
- fix: every build unit presses the helmet once right after its Upgrade press (a press takes 0.5
  percent of the time left at that moment, so a late press on a long build loses part), reads the
  end time before, at +1 and at +10 minutes, and records the count k (Steer A 14:04Z; the +10
  read may drop after five confirmed builds).
- target metric and current value: (1) builds started by a unit with a helmet press within 10
  minutes of the Upgrade press, baseline 0 of 4 (the two presses of 11-26 came 3.5 h and 53 min
  late); (2) hours cut per build, baseline 0, expected 10 to 46 h; (3) confirmed builds with 30 of
  30 helps, 2 of the 5 needed.
- verification: the building-queues records carry the helmet step and the end times; the coach
  books each build. It closes when five consecutive builds show (1) true and (2) of 10 h or more.
- status: open - the rule is adopted; the next builds are the test.

### IMP-20261124-03 - stock claims made from one read, and forensic reads without a plan reason

- source: Steer A ruling of 23:34Z; coach files `msb-test-prereg.csv`,
  `special-tab-window-1646-2258.csv`, `drag-distance-table.csv`.
- class: method
- leak: on 11-24 three statements about stock counts were wrong or had to be withdrawn, each from
  a single read or a misnamed stack: the coach's claim that a 299 box read was a misread (it was
  correct); the coach's five Backpack predictions that all missed because an earlier read was
  taken as a baseline; and the coordinator's line that the turkey TICKETS fell 7 to 1 (the
  Backpack's turkey ITEM did; the tickets were 6 before and after). Separately the day spent five
  Backpack reads (1601, 1619, 2258, 2315, 2328), each about 0.2 to 0.3 USD by Steer A's figure,
  to reconstruct what another hand did, which changed no plan.
- root cause, MEASURED: the stock moves by hands we do not see (the Mystery Supply Box stack went
  to zero twice with no inflow, 356 and 376 boxes), so one read is a point on a moving series; a
  count in a Backpack tile is named by its tile, a currency only by its own page; a pan on a
  clipped page needs a page-specific start (the margin start failed on the Backpack and the Store
  list and worked on the Weekly Pass page and the History list; the centre of a tile-gap
  crossing moved the grid 143 and 150 px with no selection, n = 2).
- fix: (a) a stock baseline is two reads close together (the 23:15Z and 23:28Z reads, 13 minutes
  apart, are the model); (b) the pan start table on the Backpack route card; (c) a currency is
  named only when read on its own page; (d) a forensic read needs a plan reason in its brief
  (a conversion, an open date, the HQ 31 start, a stock check before a cost), and the clipped rows
  are read in the same visit.
- target metric and current value: (1) stock-count retractions per day in team messages, baseline
  3 on 11-24; (2) forensic Backpack reads per day without a plan reason, baseline 3 (2258, 2315,
  2328); (3) the share of stock claims backed by two reads within the hour, baseline 0 of 3.
- verification: the daily report carries the three numbers from the coach's message log and the
  queue briefs. It closes when retractions are 0 for five consecutive days and every Backpack
  read brief names its plan reason.
- status: open - the rules are adopted; they close only when the metric moves, measured by the coach.

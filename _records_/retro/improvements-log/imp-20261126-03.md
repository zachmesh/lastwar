### IMP-20261126-03 - plan-serving units labelled snapshot or upkeep count as serving nothing

- source: watchdog pass 15:28Z (coverage 62.8 percent, margin 2.8); coach file
  `coverage-fall-2026-11-26.csv`; the exempt list `serves-exempt-list.csv`.
- class: measurement
- leak: coverage definition A fell from 66.1 percent at 12:00Z to 62.8 percent at 15:30Z. In the
  last 12 hours the helmet test (0.73 h, a builder lever of the Oil Well chain, regular
  building-queues, PL-08 and PL-17) was labelled `snapshot`, the takeover sessions (0.41 h) and
  daily-missions (0.29 h) `upkeep`; the tool counts these as serving nothing. With the helmet unit
  counted the rolling 12 h would be 63.1 percent, not 48.8.
- root cause: the serves label is set at dispatch, not by the regular's class in the exempt list,
  so a unit of a plan regular can carry a label that names no ledger row.
- fix: the Strategist decides each such label on the merits (the coach does not label to lift
  coverage); the daily-claims-income line and the exempt list file are the watchdog's fix; the
  Planner's template uses the regular's ledger row when the unit serves it.
- target metric and current value: (1) definition A at each watchdog pass, baseline 62.8 percent
  with limit 60; (2) not-ranked hours of units whose regular is a plan or income regular in the
  exempt list, baseline 0.73 h in the last 12 h; (3) serving-nothing units per day, baseline 26
  in 12 h.
- verification: the daily report carries (1) and (2) from `coverage-honest-method.py`. It closes
  when (1) stays at or above 60 for seven consecutive passes and (2) is 0.
- status: open - the coach's prior is 0.55 that definition A is below 60 at the 04:00Z pass.

# Duties - strategist

## Each day

1. Read the new measured reads (scout, operator, coach) and research reports.
2. Update the hypothesis register: confirm, retire or add; each row names a
   test, who does it and the date it is read.
3. Rewrite the 7-day plan of record: the order of reads, the next actions, what
   each waits for and the trigger that starts it.
4. Give the planner ranked levers and rules (never queue units yourself).

## Each week

Rewrite the season-level plan; draft the owner digest (plan versus actual, top
three decisions with their rationale, what the owner must decide); review the
resource policy against the coach's series.

## Decisions

A decision above one builder-day or 5 percent of a stock gets a record with the
rationale, at least two alternatives, the expected gain with its unit and
window, the cost, the evidence label, the guardrail check and a review date.

## Evidence tags

[M] measured on this account, [D] derived, [S3] three or more sources, [S1] one
or two sources, [G] guess. Challenge every number before the team acts on it.

## Interfaces

Scout reads, researcher sources, coach series, planner units, operator
actions, scribe pages, integrator landings, coordinator assignment and records,
Steer A relays to the owner. Write small files under
`_records_/<date>/strategy/`; the scribe lands the pages.

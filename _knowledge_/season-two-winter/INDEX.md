# Season 2 — Polar Storm

This account is in Season 2, checked in game on 2026-10-25 — snow map,
a base temperature readout (5 degrees shown) and a warming-help counter
on the base screen. The mechanics below are external research, consistent
across the Handbook, Tutorial and TKSL guides unless noted.

## Temperature

- The map is seven concentric zones, colder toward the Capitol — minus 15
  at the outer zone, then minus 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, and minus 80 at the
  Capitol (Handbook; Medium guide agrees zone by zone).
- Below 0, resource production falls. Below minus 20, a five-minute
  countdown starts. A base that stays below minus 20 for five minutes is
  **frozen** — no rallies and no advanced teleport while frozen.
- Defrost by holding the base above minus 20 continuously for five
  minutes, or by allies breaking the ice — five hits at five stamina each
  (Tutorial; Medium agrees).
- Blizzards drop ambient temperature sharply — minus 30 in weeks 1 to 2,
  minus 50 in week 3, minus 70 from week 4 (Handbook, single source for
  the week values). One guide describes a high-temperature state where a
  base above 40 can catch fire (TKSL), which the other guides do not
  cover; recorded as single-source.

## Heating

- **High-Heat Furnace** — the season's key building, max level 30,
  independent of HQ. Burns coal, raises base temperature and virus
  resistance. Overdrive heats far more at four times the coal; every guide
  warns to switch it off after the need passes. Upgrades take titanium
  alloy. Level 30 unlocks Military Bases (Handbook).
- **Alliance Furnace** — placed by R4 or R5, heats an area for nearby
  members, fuelled by member coal donations, and upgraded from alliance
  Rare Soil. It is the target in Rare Soil wars.
- **Recon planes from allies** can warm a base, which is the mechanic
  behind the warming-help daily on this account's base screen.

## Season resources

- **Coal** — furnace fuel. Sources include dig sites (about 120 to 300
  per hour by level), Doom Elite and polar zombie first kills, radar
  missions and passes (Handbook source table).
- **Titanium alloy** — furnace upgrade material, from Titanium Alloy
  Factories (max four, a fifth with the Weekly Pass; each next factory
  unlocks when the previous reaches level 15), first kills, fields and
  passes.
- **Rare Soil** — the alliance ranking resource. Captured cities and dig
  sites generate it continuously, so holding time matters more than a
  late capture (Medium guide; Handbook agrees on generation).

## Daily season routine (Medium guide, single source)

Check temperature and the next cold wave, review coal, collect factory
output and dig-site coal, finish season quests and distressed-survivor
radar tasks, claim the Doom Walker first-kill reward, and time large
spending with Alliance Duel days.

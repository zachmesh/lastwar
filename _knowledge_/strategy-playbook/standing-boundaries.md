## Standing boundaries

The recording rules red lines are unchanged and override this
playbook wherever they touch - no purchases, no diamond spending,
no speedup or stamina item burning to score, no research or
building starts on the agent's own initiative, no coal decisions.
The playbook times what is already allowed; it never widens it.

Dated note 2026-11-23T07:45Z (owner-delegated; decision record `_records_/2026-11-22/team-operations/2350-growth-start-rule-decision.md`): the boundary "no research or building starts on the agent's own initiative" no longer applies to RESOURCE-ONLY starts: idle builders, labs and training queues are filled from surplus when the cost is at most a third of each stock and above the reserves (food 100M, iron 150M, coin 10M, diamonds untouched). Unchanged: no purchase, diamond spend, speed-up, stamina-item burning, coal decision, PvP, diplomacy or ally exchange.

Dated note 2026-11-23T15:28Z (Steer A, standing rule after the T3 incident, relayed by the coordinator; the Go button on the check-in banner came one click from a real-money page, `_records_/2026-11-22/shop-claims/1511-queue-run.md`): (1) any test or action whose possible effect includes opening another app, a browser, a store or a payment surface needs Steer A's approval in advance, with the exit plan stated (what is pressed if it happens, what is never pressed); never click Go on the check-in banner again. (2) An unexpected foreground switch to another app stops the run at once: press nothing, answer nothing, touch nothing in the other app, tell the Coordinator within minutes. (3) Consent requests (new_app and any other) are human-only, never answered by a seat. (4) Only the operator seat and the bridge touch the desktop; every other seat uses `lsappinfo front` (read-only) at most, with no osascript, no System Events and no TCC-relevant call.

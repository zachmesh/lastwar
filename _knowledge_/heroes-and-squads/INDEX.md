# Heroes and squads

External research, not yet checked against this account's roster. Tier
lists are community consensus and shift with balance updates. Sources are
named per claim, and disagreements are kept visible.

## Types and the counter triangle

Heroes belong to one of three vehicle types — Tank, Aircraft, Missile.
Five independent guides agree on the counter cycle, worth about 20 percent
in damage dealt and taken — **Aircraft beats Tank, Tank beats Missile,
Missile beats Aircraft** (Packsify PvP guide; BlueStacks; Theria; Lootbar;
a beginner guide summary). One source, a LINE Games Aircraft Center page,
states the reverse for Aircraft. It is treated as an error in that page,
because it also contradicts the same site's own bonus table, but the
disagreement is recorded here rather than hidden.

## Formation bonus

Running heroes of one type in a squad grants a same-type bonus. Five of a
kind gives plus 20 percent HP, attack and defense (beginner guide summary;
BlueStacks; a team-composition guide). One source gives 15 percent for
four of a kind and 5 percent for three. Packsify claims up to 44 percent
effective power for a full formation; that figure appears to stack other
bonuses and is unverified. Competitive lineups often run four of a type
plus one flex hero, trading part of the bonus for a key off-type skill.

## Tier consensus at the top

Across Packsify, Sportsdunia, LDShop and OfZen the same names recur at the
top, which is the usable signal — exact tier letters differ per source.

- Tank — Kimberly (area damage carry), Marshall (support, team buffs),
  Williams (defense, team damage reduction), Murphy, Mason.
- Aircraft — DVA (single-target burst), Schuyler (crowd control), Morrison,
  Carlie (the type's frontline), Lucius.
- Missile — Tesla (energy damage), Fiona (multi-target, the Missile core),
  Adam, Swift, McGregor (taunt frontline).

Lower investment value by consensus — Stetmann, Violet and Sarah sit a
tier below the core, and Monica, Scarlett, Richard, Cage, Elsa, Farhad,
Gump, Loki, Maxwell, Ambolt, Venom, Blaz and Kane are ranked at or near the
bottom by most lists.

## Canonical squads (Packsify, cross-checked with LDShop roles)

- Tank squad — Williams, Kimberly, DVA, Schuyler, and Murphy or Fiona as
  flex. The standard first squad to build.
- Aircraft squad — Lucius, Carlie, Morrison, Schuyler, DVA. Built to
  counter Tank squads.
- Missile squad — Adam, McGregor, Fiona, Tesla, Swift. Built to counter
  Aircraft squads, and countered by Tank.

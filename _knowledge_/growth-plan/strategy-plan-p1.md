# Strategy 3 - the plan, part 1

3. THE PLAN, IN ORDER (each step: read first, act only if the preview passes the 3x rule and the reserves)

 P1 READ CAPACITY AND THE HOSPITAL (scout, now): number of Drill Grounds, level and capacity of each, Unit Count, the hospital (healed, wounded, capacity), the Drill Ground Expansion and Extra Drill Ground nodes (done, level, effect, cost, time, Valor), the promote panel. Record the exact power change when a collection happens.

 P2 BUY CAPACITY BY CAPACITY PER COIN: rank every capacity source (research nodes, Drill Ground levels) by slots per million coin from the previews; take the cheapest first; the first free lab (about 11:43Z) and the first free builder (2026-11-24T09:20Z) go to it. The owner's own coin spending decides the pace (section 7).

 P3 WHEN SLOTS OPEN: bring the HEALED troops home FIRST (free, +1,647 each), then fill the rest with new T10 from the food and iron surplus; collect every finished batch the same day.

 P4 NO FIGHTING THAT WOUNDS TROOPS while the Drill Ground is full (every wounded troop is power that cannot come back). Our units never fight; ask the owner about hand play (section 7).

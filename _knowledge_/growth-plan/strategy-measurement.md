# Strategy 6 - measurement

6. MEASUREMENT

 Total Power by category twice a day after collecting; every action logs the exact before and after. The planner's ledger ranks by power per coin and per slot-day. Weekly digest: plan versus actual, top three decisions with rationale, next week.

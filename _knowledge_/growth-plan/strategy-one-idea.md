# Strategy 2 - the one idea

2. THE ONE IDEA: POWER IS CAPPED BY HOW MANY TROOPS FIT. CAPACITY FIRST.

 Every 1,000 free slots filled with T10 = +1.647M power for 6.9M food and 6.9M iron, which we have in surplus [D]. A Drill Ground level is only +200 slots (+329K) for 39M to 70M coin, so the 5,306 backlog is 27 levels away by levels alone [D]. So the real work is to FIND THE CHEAPEST CAPACITY: how many Drill Grounds exist and at which level, what each level and each research node ("Drill Ground Expansion" 10 levels, 4.6M to 63.8M coin for the first five; "Extra Drill Ground" 61M coin plus 700 Valor if not already done) really adds in the Unit Count, and whether the hospital or marches change the count. READ BEFORE SPENDING.

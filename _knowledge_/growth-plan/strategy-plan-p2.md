# Strategy 3 - the plan, part 2

 P5 HERO BLOCK, 44%: exclusive weapons (about +0.3M per milestone, +7.5% at Lv.20; six exist on this season [S1]); then gear levels, skills in their scoring windows, decorations to Lv.3 placed, honor wall; sub-cap heroes (Lv.120 and Lv.140-145) to 150 if EXP allows (wave 2). Needs the shard, medal, ore and EXP stock reads. DO NOT convert universal hero or exclusive-weapon shards to coin.

 P6 DRONE (3.9%, headroom unknown): read the next-level preview; feed Battle Data below 80% of a level; wave 2 gives the routine.

 P7 COIN: just-in-time conversion of universal resource chests (never hero shards, medals, ore, blueprints), own intercity trucks, the weekly Gold Zombie event, radar, VIP free boxes; no plunder, no diamonds.

 P8 FILL IDLE SLOTS: labs always busy and PRESS COMPLETE the moment a node finishes; builders on the best power per slot-day; Oil Well levels 1 to 9 take food and iron only.

 P9 SEASON 3 (about 2026-12-13): replan; keep a coin and shard reserve (wave 2).

 NOT NOW: HQ 31, the Age of Oil path, gear star promotion, Wall of Honor above Lv.200, a third Tech Center.

# Strategy 4 - resources

4. RESOURCES

 Floors: food 100M, iron 150M, coin 10M, diamonds untouched, plus the duel-day reservation. Surplus food and iron go to troops (capacity-limited) and Oil Well levels; coin is rationed by measured power per million coin, capacity first.

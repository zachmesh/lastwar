### Bubble lines are structural, not counted

Ruling of Steer A, 2026-11-25 01:21Z, on the Coach's measurement. It governs lines 1 to 3 of [Base and harvest](base-and-harvest.md) (food, iron and coin bubbles) and supersedes their closing standard 'no bubble standing at the final sweep' for the zero sweeps.

**Why.** Production refills the buildings at about 448K coin, 684K food and 631K iron an hour (the Coach's rates, which reproduce the 00:49Z collect to 0.1M). A bubble stands about 16 minutes after any collect and says nothing about the day's collection. The WORLD then BASE refresh does not clear it: it is neither a stale drawing nor a claim.

**The definition.** A sweep does not count the standing bubbles. It takes the harvest unit's last collect record from the records. The line counts AT ZERO when that collect is within 3 hours of the sweep and the 'available' read after it was under 1 percent of the capacity bar. The sweep logs the bubbles as 'structural, not counted'. A line whose last collect is older than 3 hours, or that has no collect record that day, counts as not at zero and enqueues a priority 1 harvest fix-up.

**The denominator.** 20 lines (the 23 of 2026-11-24 less the three bubble lines); 17 when the three passes lines 'as directed' (a passes unit open and dispatched by direction) are also taken out by their own rule.

**The condition against a quiet re-basing.** The daily report states the denominator and this definition in its header every day and carries both versions side by side (the old 23-line count against the new). No day's figure is compared with another's on a different definition. The 2026-11-24 sweeps read 9 and 7 of 23 on the old definition.

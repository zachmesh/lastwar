# Activity card template

Copy the block into `_activities_/<activity>/INDEX.md`, keep the frontmatter of that INDEX, fill every field and stay at 300 words or fewer ([the convention](activity-playbook-card.md)).

```markdown
# <Activity> playbook card

Goal: <one sentence, what a good run achieves>.

## Route
1. <step, at most 8 in all>

## Rules
- R1 <rule>. Status: unverified. 2026-11-23. `_records_/<date>/<activity>/<HHMM>-<file>.md`
- R2 to R5 the same way; a new rule replaces the weakest.

## Failures
- <failure> - fix: <fix>. `_records_/<date>/<activity>/<file>.md`

## Open questions
- <question> - test: <what settles it>; run by <role>.

## Numbers
<one line generated by the coach from the records, never typed by hand>

## Links
- [Rule digest](rule-digest.md)
- <the newest record, the next article to read; 5 links at most>
```
